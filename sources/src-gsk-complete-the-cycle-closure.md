## Summary

The nine-year UK national inhaler take-back run by one manufacturer through
pharmacies, and why it closed: GSK said a "bespoke, standalone scheme" could not
reach the necessary scale. Useful as the counter-case to Take AIR: single-funder
schemes are fragile even when they work. Direct fetch blocked on 2026-09-07;
claims from search extracts of the NHS notice and Pharmaceutical Journal
coverage.

## Key claims

- Complete the Cycle ran 2011 to end September 2020 across UK pharmacies.
- Recovered and recycled more than 2 million inhalers.
- Carbon saving reported as equivalent to taking about 8,600 cars off UK roads
  (⚠️ company-derived figure).
- GSK's stated reason for closure: as the first national scheme of its kind it
  could not achieve scale as a bespoke standalone scheme; an industry-wide
  approach was called for.
- As of the PJ reporting, NHS England had no plans for a national inhaler
  recycling scheme.
