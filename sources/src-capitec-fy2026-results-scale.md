## Summary

Capitec's FY2026 results give the scale behind the branch network. The bank
reports 26 million active clients and 15.3 million banking-app clients, with
operating profit before tax up 25% to R22.179 billion. The earnings mix matters
more than the totals for a partnership case: personal banking is now 41% of
group headline earnings, with insurance at 27% and fintech, meaning value-added
services and Capitec Connect, at 26%. Capitec makes real money selling
non-lending services to people it already banks, which is the commercial logic a
third-party collection service would have to fit into.

## Key claims

- **26 million active clients** (reported April 2026).
- **15.3 million banking-app clients**, up 19% year on year from 12.9 million.
- **Operating profit before tax R22.179 billion**, up 25% from R17.740 billion.
- **Headline earnings per share 14,606 cents**, up 23% from 11,912 cents.
- **Value-added services and Capitec Connect income grew 38% to R6.1 billion**.
- **Net insurance income grew 38% to R5.2 billion**.
- Group headline earnings mix: **personal banking 41%, insurance 27%, fintech
  (VAS and Capitec Connect) 26%, business banking 5%**.
- **Over 6.5 million users** actively engage with the Live Better rewards
  programme.
- AI anti-fraud measures reported as saving **R673 million**.
