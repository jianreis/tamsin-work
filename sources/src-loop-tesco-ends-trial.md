## Summary

The cautionary half of the Loop story. Tesco, the largest grocer to try Loop at
scale, ended the trial in mid-2022 after 12 months online and nine months in
ten stores, with some 80,000 Loop products sold. The learning Tesco reported is
that "pre-fill" (customer collects an already-filled returnable container)
worked better than customer refilling. Direct fetch blocked on 2026-09-07;
claims from search extracts of edie and corroborating packaging trade press.

## Key claims

- Tesco's Loop trial ended June 2022 (⚠️ month single-source; year corroborated).
- Products were available to online shoppers for 12 months and in selected
  stores for nine months.
- Customers bought more than 80,000 Loop products through Tesco.
- Range: 53 branded lines (Persil, Coca-Cola, Heinz, Tetley) plus 35 own-brand
  essentials, pre-filled.
- Deposit per container, refunded on return.
- Tesco found the pre-fill model more successful than in-store refill; COVID
  disruption to supply chains and habits is cited as context.
