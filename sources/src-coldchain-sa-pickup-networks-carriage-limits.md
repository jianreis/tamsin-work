## Summary

What the existing South African pickup rails will and will not carry, which
constrains any partner-collection concept that plans to ride on them rather than
build its own. Pargo's shipping rules prohibit all liquids outright, and place
perishable food and perishable goods on the excluded list, meaning Pargo will carry
them but accepts no liability for loss, damage or late delivery. Pargo also states
it does not ship perishable goods. Dis-Chem already operates Pargo pickup points,
so this is a live commercial relationship, not a hypothetical.

Prescription medicines are not named either way in the published Pargo material.
That silence is itself a finding: a pharmacy pushing scripts through a general
parcel network is operating outside a documented carriage category, and the liquid
prohibition alone would exclude a large slice of chronic dispensing (suspensions,
insulin pens, inhaler propellant packs vary by carrier interpretation).

PUDO lockers are open 24/7 across roughly 1,500 to 2,000 sites at malls, petrol
stations and similar. No operating temperature specification is published. A user
report notes that a PUDO collection point had no power during load-shedding, which
is a signal rather than evidence but points at the right question for a
conditioned-locker design.

## Key claims

- **Pargo prohibited list** (will not carry): tobacco, alcohol, money, bullion,
  credit and pre-paid cards, jewellery, watches, precious stones, furs, treasury
  notes, securities, stamps, explosives, livestock, weapons, **any liquids**, and all
  toxic substances.
- **Pargo excluded list** (carried, no compensation for loss, damage or late
  delivery): badly packaged goods, antiques, **perishable food, perishable goods**,
  glass items, non-flatpack furniture.
- **Pargo does not ship perishable goods** including foods, livestock, or items over
  15 kg.
- **Prescription medicines are not explicitly addressed** in Pargo's published
  prohibited or excluded lists. `⚠️ UNVERIFIED` - a secondary courier-comparison
  site asserts medications including prescription are prohibited on PUDO-style
  networks; this could not be confirmed against Pargo or PUDO primary material.
- Pargo returns uncollected parcels to sender after **8 days**.
- **Dis-Chem operates Pargo pickup points** (dischem.co.za/pargo-pickup-points).
- **PUDO** (The Courier Guy): 1,500 to 2,000+ lockers, open 24/7, sited at malls and
  petrol stations, max 20 kg. **No published operating-temperature specification.**
- User signal (Tier D, treat as sentiment not fact): a PUDO collection point locker
  reported as having no power during load-shedding.
