## Summary

The largest current pharmacy-chain blister recycling scheme. Boots piloted
in-store blister collection in 100+ London and South East stores from February
2024, expanded to 800+ UK stores in February 2025, and pays loyalty points for
returns. Processing partner MYGroup separates foil from plastic. It is an
in-store drop-off scheme for empty original manufacturer blisters; it does not
touch dispensed medicine or take anything back through delivery. Direct fetch
blocked on 2026-09-07; claims from search extracts of the Boots newsroom and
The Pharmaceutical Journal.

## Key claims

- Pilot launched February 2024 in more than 100 stores (London and South East).
- Expanded to more than 800 UK stores, announced 11 February 2025.
- Customers return any empty medicine or vitamin blister pack to in-store boxes.
- Boots Advantage Card members earn 100 points (worth GBP 1) for every five
  empty blister packs, on a GBP 5+ spend.
- Recycler MYGroup separates plastic and foil; foil re-enters the aluminium
  stream; plastic becomes "MYBoard" for furniture, playground equipment and
  construction.
- MYGroup reported processing more than seven tonnes of blister packs since the
  2024 launch (⚠️ single-source, partner-stated).
