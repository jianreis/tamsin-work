## Summary

Amazon's own description of what the PillPack sachet service physically ships:
PET packets on a roll, a recyclable cardboard dispenser box, an optional
reusable "premium" dispenser, and a candid admission that not every packet is
recyclable. No take-back or return programme is described. Refrigerated items
ship separately in temperature-protective packaging. Direct fetch blocked on
2026-09-07; claims from search extracts of the Amazon and PillPack help pages.

## Key claims

- Packets are polyethylene terephthalate (PET), described as food-safe,
  BPA-free and waterproof.
- The standard dispenser is described as "100% recyclable"; a "premium
  dispenser" (US$15, blue or purple, with travel pouch) is offered as a reusable
  alternative the customer keeps.
- "Most" shipping materials are recyclable; a typical PillPack customer is said
  to use less plastic than with conventional pill bottles (company claim, no
  figure).
- Some individual packets are recyclable and others are not; the company says
  it is testing 100% recyclable options (company statement, undated).
- No customer take-back or return of dispensers or packets is described.
- Amazon Pharmacy ships refrigerated medication separately in packaging designed
  to hold a safe temperature; no return of cold packaging is described.

## Verbatim excerpts

> "Some of our individual packets are recyclable while others aren't, but we're actively testing 100% recyclable options." (help page, as extracted)

> "If your medication requires refrigeration, it will be shipped separately in packaging designed to keep medication at a safe temperature." (Amazon Pharmacy shipping help, as extracted)
