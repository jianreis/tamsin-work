## Summary

Working deployments where a pickup locker does carry refrigerated prescriptions.
Denver Health runs three automated contactless prescription lockers and states that
most medications stocked at its pharmacy, refrigerated ones included, can be
dispensed through them. St. Luke's lockers hold both room-temperature and
refrigerated bins, with items sealed in tamper-proof bags.

The qualifier is the one that decides the Dis-Chem question. Both are **on-site
will-call lockers**: inside or adjacent to the dispensing pharmacy, on hospital
power, inside a climate-controlled building, with pharmacy staff metres away and
short dwell times. Neither is a remote unattended locker in a forecourt. They prove
the locker form factor is compatible with cold chain when the surrounding
infrastructure is a hospital, not that a distributed retail locker network is.

## Key claims

- **Denver Health**: three automated contactless prescription pharmacy lockers (two
  at the Outpatient Medical Center Pharmacy, one at the Webb Center for Primary Care
  Pharmacy). **Most medications stocked at the pharmacy, including refrigerated
  medications, can be dispensed through the lockers.**
- **St. Luke's Health System**: medication lockers include bins for both
  room-temperature and refrigerated medications, sealed in tamper-proof bags;
  operates as an automated will-call system with prescriptions prepared in the
  pharmacy and moved to the locker.
- Both are located at the dispensing site, not remote from it.
- Patient interaction is a scan plus a date-of-birth confirmation, typically under
  30 seconds.
