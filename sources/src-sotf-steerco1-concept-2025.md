# Summary

The SteerCo #1 pack (pre-read dated 6 Nov, presentation 7 Nov 2025) that
validated the initial Store of the Future concept. Produced after four
executive workshops (14-28 Oct 2025: vision & customer needs; in-store service
offering & brand; category leadership; retail & priority customer experience).
Covers the "T" store architecture, dispensary/clinic/cover/hub design choices
with rationales, category strategy, retail innovations, and the roadmap to
Melrose Arch. **Reliability A for what was proposed and why; the customer
claims inside it are workshop-generated and rate C** — see the evidence
section below.

# Key claims

## Programme frame
- SOTF is "the physical manifestation of Dis-Chem's brand aligning with the
  Better Health ambition" with 8 roles: (1) category strategy aligned to
  Better Health; (2) expanded health funding & services to strengthen Health
  authority; (3) guide/educate across the omnichannel journey; (4) experiential
  offerings; (5) elevated in-store experience via digital; (6) drive Better
  Rewards adoption; (7) spotlight strategic partners/vendors; (8) social agenda.
- 8 success metrics: category share/sales; clinic share; ecosystem
  cross-usage; operational efficiency (cost-to-serve); digital adoption;
  Better Rewards enrolment; innovation effectiveness; social impact.
- Store architecture = the "T": Cover + Dispensary + Clinic across the back,
  Hub and Innovation aisle down the centre, front-shop retail either side.

## Key design decisions and stated rationales (dispensary)
- Pharmacy at the back of the store — rationale: "Maintain the T concept and
  drive customers to the store".
- Decoupled script submission and collection — rationale: "Allow for roaming
  opportunities for customers" and pick-and-pack consolidation efficiencies.
- Three submission routes (counter, digital kiosk, app); digital queue number
  collected at Hub before any interaction — rationale: minimised queue "with
  roaming time for customers".
- Unified OTC + script flow; payment at dispensary for medication/OTC —
  rationale: "minimises requirements for cage", reduces touchpoints.
- Lockers held back: "challenging economics and limited evidence of successful
  24/7 pharmacy"; 48h pickup window mooted "to encourage prompt collection".
- The internal pre-read words principle 1 as "drive cross-sell with front shop"
  and lists dispensary location as a strength that "maximises cross-sell /
  opportunistic sales"; the shared (vShare) version softens both to "drive
  customers towards front shop" / "maximises exposure to front-shop".

## Clinic, Cover, Hub
- Clinic: nurse-led services; triage room staffed by auxiliary so nurses
  "practice at top of license"; admin moved to Hub receptionist; EHR
  integration; e-script direct to dispensary. Current-state pains listed
  (absent receptionist, paper registration, no EHR, unclear pricing).
- Cover: in-person financial advisor prioritised "due to conversion rate";
  awareness triggers across dispensary/clinic/front-shop/Hub/app (till-slip
  "you could have saved x" prompts, pharmacist triggers on chronic scripts).
- Hub: front door to the ecosystem; "conversion engine for ecosystem
  adoption"; cross-trained staff; ticketing anchor; no compromise on a
  physical person at every store size.

## Category strategy
- Captainship / play strong / participate / deprioritise-exit framework;
  non-health FMCG exits worth ~R455m plus home fragrance (R16m), baby
  once-offs (R40m); captainship = 53% of revenue (R10.4bn) on 34% of space.

## Customer-evidence basis (for the research question)
- Customer needs derive from four **workshop-defined personas** (Nandi 32,
  Lerato 74, Andrea 43, Thabo 25) built in Workshop #1 — no primary research
  study is cited for them.
- One unattributed line: "Consumer research notes high appetite for digital
  integration across journey (booking to consult)" (clinic section).
- Dispensary pain-point slide is "PRELIMINARY, FOR DISCUSSION" with no source.
- Digital innovation prioritisation sourced to "Expert interviews, Desk
  research; Market reports".
- The Jan 2026 SOTF customer interview study
  [[src-dc-store-future-interviews-2026]] post-dates this SteerCo by ~11 weeks:
  the concept was validated by the SteerCo before any dedicated customer
  research on it existed.
