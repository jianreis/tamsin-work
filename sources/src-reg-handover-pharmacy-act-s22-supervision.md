# Pharmacy Act s22 - continuous personal supervision

## Summary

The supervision rule that any unmanned collection point has to be measured
against. Section 22 requires a pharmacy to be conducted under the *continuous
personal supervision of a pharmacist*, and section 22(4)-(5) attaches that duty
to a named Responsible Pharmacist who answers to Council for acts done and acts
omitted in that pharmacy. The rule bites on the **pharmacy**, not on every
downstream point where medicine changes hands, which is why the whole
locker question turns on whether the collection point is a pharmacy or is
merely the end of a delivery leg. The Act does not answer that; nothing found in
this session defines a collection point, a locker or an off-site holding
facility either way.

Related sources already in the vault cover the supervision tiers for pharmacy
support personnel (direct vs indirect personal supervision, regulation 12 of the
Regulations Relating to the Practice of Pharmacy) - see
[[src-reg-pharmacy-act-53-1974]].

## Key claims

- A pharmacy must be conducted under the **continuous personal supervision of a
  pharmacist**, in accordance with good pharmacy practice, subject to prescribed
  conditions (s22).
- SAPC registers a **Responsible Pharmacist** in terms of s22(4) and s22(5); that
  pharmacist is answerable to Council for acts performed **and for the omission
  of any act required to be performed** in that pharmacy.
- Good Pharmacy Practice is obligatory through a stack of instruments, not one:
  **s35A of the Pharmacy Act, regulation 20(1) of the Regulations Relating to the
  Practice of Pharmacy, regulation 7(a) of the Regulations Relating to the
  Ownership and Licensing of Pharmacies, and regulation 18(7)(b) of the General
  Regulations under the Medicines Act 101 of 1965.**
- The supervision duty is framed around the pharmacy as a licensed premises.
  Nothing located in this session extends it in terms to a remote collection
  point, nor exempts one.

## Verbatim excerpts

> "A pharmacy shall, subject to such conditions as may be prescribed, be
> conducted under the continuous personal supervision of a pharmacist, in
> accordance with good pharmacy practice as determined …"
> (s22; extraction truncated at source)

> "A pharmacy must be conducted under the continuous personal supervision of a
> pharmacist, who shall be responsible to Council for any acts performed, and
> omission of any act required to be performed in that pharmacy."
> (SAPC, criteria for registration of a Responsible Pharmacist, on s22(4)-(5))

> "Good Pharmacy Practice is obligatory in terms of section 35A of the Pharmacy
> Act 53 of 1974, as amended, regulation 20(1) of the Regulations Relating to the
> Practice of Pharmacy and regulation 7(a) of the Regulations Relating to the
> Ownership and Licensing of Pharmacies published in terms of the Pharmacy Act as
> well as regulation 18(7)(b) of the General Regulations published in terms of
> the Medicines and Related Substances Act 101 of 1965, as amended."
