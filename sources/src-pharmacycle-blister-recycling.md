## Summary

Australian pharmacy-funded blister recycling service, and the only scheme found
that explicitly accepts emptied multi-dose adherence packs (Webster-pak,
SureMed dose administration aids) alongside manufacturer blisters. Pharmacies
pay a service fee; drop-off is free to the public; material is shredded and
separated into aluminium and plastic at a Sydney plant. Vendor self-description,
so tier C. Direct fetch blocked on 2026-09-07; claims from search extracts of
Pharmacycle pages and RUM's announcement of the partnership.

## Key claims

- Accepts all brands of empty medicinal blister packs and emptied dose
  administration aids such as Webster-pak and SureMed; all medication must be
  removed first.
- 900+ collection points across Australia (vendor figure; RUM cited 200+
  pharmacies in its first year).
- Free to the public; pharmacies pay a service fee covering administration,
  logistics, processing and reporting; also funded by industry sponsors and
  government bodies.
- Process: check-in and weigh, inspect for residual medicine and contamination,
  pre-shred, proprietary separation of plastic from aluminium in Sydney.
