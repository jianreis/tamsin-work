# Summary

Three workshop decks (Day 1: vision, new app, dispensary; Day 2: clinic,
cover, Health Hub; Day 3: digital, tech, go-to-market, operations, people &
culture) from the 3-day working session that locked the Melrose Arch MVP
against the target state, roughly three months before opening. Deck footers
say "2025" but content dates (SAP workshop 23 Feb 2026, IT deck dated 24 Feb
2026, training milestones March-April 2026) confirm February 2026. Tamsin is a
named owner of the Day-1 Dispensary session. Reliability A for decisions and
scope; roadmap dates are plans, not outcomes.

# Key claims

## Melrose Arch MVP vs target state (per asset)
- **Dispensary, live at Melrose:** separate submission and pickup counters;
  unified OTC + script; automated Qmatic ticketing (digital + printed);
  omnichannel submission (WhatsApp, calls, Adherence) centralised with
  collection only; dedicated pick-and-pack operators with SLA prioritisation.
  Deferred: in-store digital script submission (H2 2026); payment at
  dispensary (2028, with Bricks/POS update); Rowa automation (volume
  insufficient); **pickup lockers deprioritised short-term**.
- **Clinic, live:** waiting area + pre-consultation room + consultation room;
  reception/check-in/payment at Hub; nurse-led with virtual GP access;
  e-script direct to dispensary. Consent/admin stays with the Clinic Care
  Assistant at opening due to CRM-Healthforce integration gaps.
- **Cover, live:** dedicated consultation room; card payment at Hub and with
  advisor; booking at Hub (in-store only for MVP, not app/web).
- **Hub, live:** cross-trained Hub Advisors + Hub Administrator;
  ticketing/booking kiosks and tablets; loyalty sign-up; selected self-service
  cases. Deferred: store navigation (cut from MVP), omnichannel ordering and
  gamified experiential screens (H1 2027), digital script submission.
- **Front shop, live:** 1 health experiential area (skin-care AR later);
  D-Media gondola ends; drive aisle + seasonal tables; returns/exchanges at
  checkout. Deferred: ESL/personalised pricing (H2 2026-H2 2027), camera
  sensors, AI skin analysis (H1 2027).

## Operating-model decisions (Day 1-2)
- Three components to test in SOTF: (1) remove noise — centralise
  WhatsApp/email/call-in orders to the Adherence Centre; (2) ticketing
  (workflow management); (3) decoupling (dispense vs fulfilment).
- One ticket number spans the whole store visit; queue management framed as
  "slotted delivery of service, eliminating walk-in concept across all
  assets"; bookings prioritised over walk-ins; emergencies (Hub Advisor
  triage, first-aid trained) over bookings.
- Late-arrival rule: >10 minutes late forfeits the slot, treated as walk-in.
- Clinic naming: Clinic Care Assistant, Nursing Practitioner,
  Pre-Consultation Room; clinic scripts prioritised in the dispensary queue;
  30-minute slots to be tested.
- "Cage of Shame" replaced by Magna bags; ID verification added after fraud
  cases; OTC upsell at submission, not collection.
- No direct WhatsApp number for Melrose Arch — WhatsApp/calls centralised and
  primarily outbound.

## Delivery machinery
- McKinsey runs governance (weekly sponsor check-ins, sprint planning, JIRA
  work parcels); QuantumBlack behind AI concepts; TDC&Co store design; One
  Digital screens; Q-Matic ticketing; Healthforce clinic system; Braze
  notifications; Vexall dashboards; SAP CRM/CDC; GKPOS.
- New app rebuild in parallel (Tribe 1, 6 squads, ~90 builders): app re-architected
  (load 25s → 1.5s), Better Rewards in-app, OCR script checkout, slotted
  delivery groundwork; radical-innovation concepts (AI chat commerce, social
  commerce) explored beyond MVP.
- Staffing model: 47 headcount for the full SOTF store; new roles Hub Advisor
  (x2) and Hub Administrator; training milestones 16 Mar - end Apr 2026;
  "Melrose Hype" campaign from 7 May 2026; Smollan bolstering at opening.
- Go-to-market: Melrose Arch is the explicit "test-and-learn pilot" and
  "proof point for future openings".

## Customer-evidence basis
- No customer research is cited anywhere in the three decks. The Jan 2026
  SOTF interview study [[src-dc-store-future-interviews-2026]] (which tested
  these very journey concepts with 12 customers a month earlier) is not
  referenced. Decisions are argued from operations, cost, tech feasibility
  and commercial rationale; a "customer dry-run pre-opening" and a "customer
  education workstream" are named as next steps.
