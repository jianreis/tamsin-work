## Sheet: Glossary
Glossary of Terms (Dis-Chem Integrated Health Ecosystem)
This glossary standardises terminology across clinic, pharmacy, digital, insurance, and partner channels. It ensures consistent language in capability models, requirements, and Accenture collaboration.
Clinician | Definition: A healthcare professional who provides direct clinical assessment, diagnosis, treatment, or care management. At Dis‑Chem this includes nurses, doctors, and virtual care clinicians (GPs and specialists).
Does NOT include pharmacists, whose regulatory scope focuses on dispensing, counselling, adherence, and chronic support.
Why it matters: Prevents ambiguity in capability ownership and clinical workflow design.
Pharmacist | Definition: A licensed healthcare professional responsible for medication dispensing, counselling, medicine safety, adherence support, chronic medication continuity, OTC triage, and pharmacy‑driven VBC tasks.
Not classified as a clinician in South Africa.
Role in ecosystem: Owns pharmacy‑specific channels and contributes to care pathways.
Provider | Definition: Any professional or organisation delivering healthcare services, including clinics, virtual doctors, specialists, pharmacists, labs, and partner providers.
Used broadly to include both clinicians and pharmacists.
Patient | Definition: A person receiving clinical or virtual care through Dis‑Chem Clinics, Videomed, or specialist services.
Customer | Definition: Any person engaging with Dis‑Chem for retail, pharmacy, clinic, digital, or loyalty services.
A customer may or may not be a patient.
