## Summary

Dis-Chem relaunched its loyalty programme as Better Rewards on 21 October 2025
and named Capitec its only banking partner. Members get 10% instant discount at
the till on more than 140 brands; Capitec clients paying with a Capitec card get
a further 5%, so 15%. Dis-Chem attributes 550,000 new Better Rewards customers
to the Capitec tie-in. This upgrades the 2021 cash-back arrangement into an
exclusive: Capitec is the bank inside Dis-Chem's loyalty economics, and the
discount is applied at the till in real time rather than paid back later.

## Key claims

- **Better Rewards launched 21 October 2025**, replacing the previous Dis-Chem
  Benefit programme.
- Members receive **10% instant discount at the till** across **more than 140
  brands and over 10,000 products**.
- **Capitec clients receive an additional 5%, for a 15% instant discount**, by
  paying with Capitec.
- Capitec is described as **Dis-Chem's only banking partner**.
- Dis-Chem reported **550,000 new Better Rewards customers** attributed to the
  Capitec deal.
- Holders of **Dis-Chem Health medical insurance, gap cover or Dis-Chem Life**
  policies qualify for a Better Rewards discount of **at least 20%**.
- The discount is **applied instantly at the point of sale**, a change from the
  2021 model of monthly cash back into a savings account.
