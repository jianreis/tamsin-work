## Summary

Algramo (Santiago, Chile) is the emerging-market reuse precedent: RFID-tagged
durable containers refilled from in-store dispensers (including Walmart Chile)
or from electric tricycles at the door, paid by the gram through an app, with
a credit each time the same container is refilled. Relevant to SA because it
was built for low-income neighbourhood stores, not for premium shoppers. Direct
fetch blocked on 2026-09-07; claims from search extracts of WEF and
corroborating pages.

## Key claims

- Founded 2012 in Santiago; operates through 2,000+ family-owned neighbourhood
  stores reaching 325,000+ customers (⚠️ figures single-source, 2021).
- RFID-tagged containers; refill via in-store vending-style stations (Walmart,
  Aldi named) or an electric tricycle delivering to the door; ordering and
  payment through an app.
- Customers save up to 30% versus packaged product; each refill of an existing
  container earns a credit equivalent to an 11.5% discount (⚠️ single-source).
- Brand partners: Unilever (Omo, Quix), Nestle Purina; Walmart as retail partner.
- Algramo collects, cleans and refills containers; end-of-life containers are
  recycled.

## Verbatim excerpts

> "Algramo collects empty packaging containers, which are subsequently cleaned and refilled for delivery back to the stores, and at the end of their life these containers are recycled." (Circle Economy summary, as extracted)
