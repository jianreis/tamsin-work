## Summary

Trade-press account of TerraCycle's Loop, the best-known consumer returnable
packaging platform: branded products sold in durable containers against a
refundable deposit, returned via tote pickup or in-store drop-off, washed and
refilled. By end-2022 Loop had wound down its standalone home-delivery
e-commerce model and moved into physical retail (Kroger's Fred Meyer, Giant
Food, Walmart in Arkansas, Carrefour, Aeon, Tesco, Woolworths AU). Direct fetch
blocked by the egress proxy on 2026-09-07; claims from search extracts of Waste
Dive, Retail Dive and Waste Management Review pages.

## Key claims

- Loop model: consumer pays a deposit per container, refunded on return;
  empties go into a Loop tote for pickup or are dropped at a store; containers
  are cleaned, refilled and re-sold.
- Retail partners named: Kroger and Walgreens (US), Carrefour (France), Aeon
  (Japan), Tesco and McDonald's (UK), Woolworths (Australia).
- Loop ended 2022 with about 150 active retail locations worldwide, around
  three dozen in the US (⚠️ single-source figure).
- The standalone Loop e-commerce (home-delivery) programme was sunset in favour
  of in-store pickup and drop-off.
- Woolworths Australia launched Loop mid-2021: products in durable branded
  packaging delivered in a reusable tote; empties collected from home or dropped
  in store to be cleaned, refilled and reused. Current status of the AU
  programme ⚠️ UNVERIFIED.
