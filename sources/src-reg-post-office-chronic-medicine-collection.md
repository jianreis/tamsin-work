# The State already hands chronic medicine over at 342 post offices

## Summary

An official government statement confirming that patients collect chronic clinic
medication at **342 post offices** across every province except the Western Cape,
aimed at people who live or work closer to a post office than to a government
clinic. Post offices are open until 17:00 on weekdays and on Saturday mornings,
which is a wider window than most clinics manage.

For the locker question this matters because a post office counter is not a
pharmacy, the person behind it is not registered with SAPC, and the medicine
being handed over is dispensed scheduled medicine. The State has therefore
already answered the practical question in its own favour, publicly and at
scale, without any recorded objection from the pharmacy regulator that this
session could find. The precedent is procedural rather than legal: it shows
tolerance, not authorisation.

## Key claims

- Chronic clinic medication may be collected at **342 post offices**, in **all
  provinces except the Western Cape**.
- The service targets patients who **live or work closer to a post office than to
  a government clinic**.
- Post office opening hours are **until 17:00 on weekdays and Saturday
  mornings**.
- The statement was issued on **2 June 2022**, in a context of COVID-19 and
  flood displacement.
- The medicine is centrally dispensed under CCMDD and delivered pre-labelled and
  sealed; see [[src-reg-ccmdd-pickup-point-handover]].

## Verbatim excerpts

> "This collection service is available in all provinces, except the Western
> Cape, at 342 post offices. The service is aimed at patients who live or work

> open until 17:00 on weekdays and Saturday mornings."
