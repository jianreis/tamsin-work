## 1. Payment methods (in-store and online), accounts

**Facts**
- Dis-Chem runs an official payment-options page. Search summary: online options include "credit cards, digital wallets, bank transfers, buy-now-pay-later services, and accepts Mobicred and RCS." [https://www.dischem.co.za/payment-options] (A, n.d.)
- **Mobicred works in-store as well as online.** Customer flow: open the Mobicred app, tap "Purchase", enter the amount, confirm by OTP, and the app generates a **wiCode** that the cashier keys in. "To pay in-store at a Dis-Chem, you'll first need to download the Mobicred App" (search snippet). [https://www.dischem.co.za/mobicred] (A, n.d.)
- **Discovery Miles as tender:** accepted online (choose Discovery Miles at checkout, pay any balance with Discovery Card or Visa/Mastercard) [https://www.dischem.co.za/discovery-miles] (A, n.d.). In-store it runs as a voucher or QR code generated in the Discovery Bank app ("Pay In-store" → enter rand amount → "Generate voucher" → show cashier). This needs an active Vitality Money status. [https://www.discovery.co.za/vitality/how-to-spend-miles] (A for Discovery's own rules, n.d.)
- **Discovery Card earn:** "earn up to 10x more Discovery Miles depending on their Vitality Status". Separately, Vitality HealthyCare gives up to 25% cash back on HealthyCare items. [https://www.discovery.co.za/credit-card/dischem] [https://www.dischem.co.za/discovery-healthycare] (A, n.d.)
- **eBucks: the spend partnership ended.** FNB ended the eBucks earn/spend partnership with Dis-Chem on 31 March 2020. eBucks can still buy discounted Dis-Chem vouchers. [https://businesstech.co.za/news/banking/371718/dis-chem-and-ebucks-end-partnership/] (B, ~2020) [https://www.ebucks.com/web/eBucks/partners/dis-chem.jsp] (A-for-FNB, n.d.). Historically eBucks could pay for "prescription and over-the-counter medication … clinic visits" [https://www.ebucks.com/web/eBucks/aboutus/pressreleases/2011/201106dischempartnership.jsp] (A, 2011; historical only).
- **Gift cards / vouchers:** "Virtual Gift Cards (Wicodes) can be used both online and in-store, but … physical (store bought) gift cards cannot be redeemed online." Online, "Apply Gift Card" also takes Discovery Vitality / Momentum Rewards voucher codes. [https://www.dischem.co.za/site-faqs] (A, n.d.)
- **Other bank-reward cashback at Dis-Chem:** Absa Rewards (up to 30% cash back on selected health and beauty spend, linked to the Dis-Chem loyalty card) [https://www.absa.co.za/personal/bank/absa-rewards/cash-rewards-from-partners/earn-partners/dis-chem/] (A-for-Absa, n.d.; wording still refers to the old "Loyalty Benefit Programme", so it may be stale). Multiply is mentioned in a search summary at up to 12%, with no primary URL found (unverified).
- **QR / Scan-to-Pay:** a search summary says Dis-Chem accepts Capitec Scan to Pay (SnapScan / Zapper QR). The supporting pages are Capitec's generic Scan-to-Pay launch articles, which **do not name Dis-Chem clearly**. [https://businesstech.co.za/news/banking/451246/capitec-launches-scan-to-pay-functionality-in-its-app-including-snapscan-and-zapper/] (B, 2020). **Treat as unverified.**
- **Pay on Collection (online order, pay in store):** "place your order and only pay when you come in store to collect"; "you will be taken to a designated counter to pay for your order"; "You will not be able to place more than one Payment on Collection orders at a time"; a proxy can collect with the order number plus the customer's name, surname and ID. [https://www.dischem.co.za/pay-on-collection] (A, n.d.)
- **Returns / refunds:** return for "credit, refund, exchange or replacement within 14 days of purchase" with the original tax invoice / till slip. [https://www.dischem.co.za/returns-refunds] (A, n.d.)
- **Baby City** takes Capitec cards (with the Better Rewards boost), Mobicred and the Dis-Chem loyalty card. [https://www.babycity.co.za/capitec] [https://www.babycity.co.za/better-rewards] (A, n.d.)

**Not found (turn into questions)**
- An official, complete **in-store** tender list: tap/contactless, Apple / Google / Samsung Pay, Capitec Pay, SnapScan / Zapper, Payflex, HappyPay, cash limits.
- **Payflex / HappyPay**: no Dis-Chem page found. Only Mobicred and RCS are confirmed.
- **Cash-back / cash withdrawal at till**: no evidence either way.
- **Medical savings cards** (scheme-branded debit cards) used as tender at the front till: nothing found.
- **Dis-Chem store / credit / corporate / staff accounts**: no Dis-Chem-branded credit or store account found. Staff-purchase and account rules are not public.
- Whether **dispensary co-payments and cash scripts are paid at the dispensary or the front till**: not documented publicly.

> **Inference:** tender types show at least three integration patterns: card-present EFT; wiCode / voucher-keyed (Mobicred, virtual gift cards, Discovery Miles in-store voucher); and card-linked offers (Capitec boost, Absa, Discovery). Each probably carries its own reconciliation and settlement stream.

## 2. Loyalty: Better Rewards and the Capitec boost

**Facts**
- **Launch:** Better Rewards "will go live on 21 October 2025", with instant savings at the till. [https://businesstech.co.za/news/business/839545/new-dis-chem-rewards-programme-launching-in-south-africa-with-a-big-boost-for-capitec-customers/] (B, Oct 2025) [https://lifestyleandtech.co.za/company-news/article/2025-10-09/dis-chems-new-loyalty-programme-drives-investment-into-customer-healthcare] (C, 2025-10-09)
- **Mechanic:** it replaces Dis-Chem's 23-year-old benefit card. "Instead of accumulating points for future use, you now receive instant discounts at the till." Base 10% off qualifying brands; "Discounts will apply even to promotional items." [https://www.dischem.co.za/better-reward] (A, n.d.) [https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-10-20-inside-dis-chems-prescription-for-better-rewards/] (B, 2025-10-20)
- **Brand count keeps growing:** 140+ brands / 10,000+ products at launch; 170+ brands / 11,000+ products at 100 days [https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/dis-chem-saves-consumers-r350m-in-just-100-days-through-revamped-better-rewards-840843a] (B, ~Jan/Feb 2026); the online account page says "180+ brands" [https://www.dischem.co.za/site-faqs] (A).
- **Legacy points:** old Benefit points "are available to use for six months" after launch (≈ to April 2026), after which the programme is "fully … instant discounts at the till". [https://www.dischem.co.za/better-reward] (A)
- **Pharmacy Boost:** extra 5% off qualifying brands for 30 days each time a member fills an acute or chronic script or buys OTC medicine. [https://www.dischem.co.za/better-reward] (A) [https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-10-20-inside-dis-chems-prescription-for-better-rewards/] (B)
- **Insurance-linked tiers:** Dis-Chem Health, Dis-Chem Life and gap-cover policyholders get at least 20%. A better Health Rating (from an annual free HealthCheck in a Dis-Chem clinic) unlocks "up to 100% off on qualifying brands, capped at R3 000 per month." [https://dischemlife.co.za/better-rewards/boost-your-rewards/] [https://dischemhealth.co.za/dis-chem-better-rewards/] (A, n.d.)
- **Capitec boost:** "+5% on all Better Rewards brands every time you pay with your linked Capitec card". The member must be registered for Better Rewards **and link the Dis-Chem card in the Capitec banking app**. Works in-store and online, and at Baby City. [https://www.dischem.co.za/capitec] (A) [https://www.capitecbank.co.za/rewards/spend-better/dis-chem/] (A-for-Capitec) [https://www.babycity.co.za/capitec] (A)
- Capitec also advertises **1% cash back** when paying with a Capitec card at Dis-Chem (search summary of Capitec's page; may predate or sit alongside the boost). [https://www.capitecbank.co.za/rewards/spend-better/dis-chem/] (A-for-Capitec, n.d.)
- **Registration channels:** WhatsApp, website, in-store, app. A physical card is available in-store straight away, or a virtual card on registering online. [https://www.dischem.co.za/site-faqs] (A)
- **Results:** R350m returned in 100 days, "matching the full-year value historically delivered by its previous loyalty programme" [bizcommunity, above] (B). R410m in 17 weeks, plus 550,000 new shoppers (not seen in the prior 12 months) attributed to the Capitec partnership [https://businesstech.co.za/news/business/851650/capitec-deal-gives-dis-chem-550000-new-customers/] (B, ~Feb 2026). FY2026: total income margin rose despite the programme ("did not compromise on total income margin") [https://supermarket.co.za/index.php/retailer-trading-results/7949-dis-chem-reports-strong-core-retail-performance-and-market-share-gains-continues-to-drive-long-term-healthcare-ecosystem-strategy] (B, ~May 2026).
- Better Rewards was designed in **X, bigly labs**, Dis-Chem's innovation hub (R300m+ invested last year). [https://www.moneyweb.co.za/news/companies-and-deals/why-dis-chem-is-spending-hundreds-of-millions-to-disrupt-itself/] (B, ~Jun 2026)

**Not found**
- How the till applies stacked discounts (base 10% + Pharmacy Boost + Capitec 5% + insurer tier), and whether the Capitec 5% is applied at POS or settled afterwards via the bank / card-linked-offer rail.
- Who funds each discount layer (brand partner vs Dis-Chem vs Capitec) and how brand-funded discounts are reclaimed from suppliers (a finance recon flow).
- Whether Better Rewards discounts apply to **dispensary lines** or scheme-paid items, and how a discount interacts with a medical aid claim amount.
- How the R3,000/month cap is tracked across channels.

> **Inference:** brand-funded instant discounts imply a supplier-claim / rebate receivable. That fits the "rebates" and "vendor reconciliation" hints in section 5, and is worth asking the debtors team about.

## 3. Medical aid at Dis-Chem

**Facts: DSP / network status**
- **Discovery Health Medical Scheme:** Dis-Chem is in the **MedXpress network** for chronic medicine (Priority, Saver, Smart Saver, Smart, Core). Using a non-network pharmacy can carry a **20% co-payment** under Regulation 8(2). [https://www.discovery.co.za/wcm/discoverycoza/assets/medical-aid/benefit-information/2026/cover-for-medicine-and-treatment-of-chronic-conditions.pdf] (A, 2026 benefit document)
- **GEMS:** Dis-Chem Pharmacies is listed among the chronic network pharmacies, alongside Pharmacy Direct, Clicks Retail and Clicks Direct Medicine. [https://www.gems.gov.za/-/media/Project/Documents/Member-Guides/2024/February/MedYEM1095-GEMS-Chronic-Medicine-Member-Guide-for-2024-v9.pdf] (A, 2024 guide; 2026 status not confirmed)
- **POLMED:** Pharmacy DSP network includes Medipost, Pharmacy Direct, Clicks and Dis-Chem. [https://zenzele.metropolitanhrm.co.za/…] (A-ish, via Metropolitan portal, n.d.)
- **Medihelp:** Dis-Chem Direct is DSP for HIV/AIDS medicine; Dis-Chem is DSP for preventive-care health assessments; **Dis-Chem Oncology** is a DSP for PMB cancer medicine. [https://www.medihelp.co.za/about/partners] (A, n.d.)
- **Fedhealth:** on myFED and flexiFED Savvy, chronic medicine must come from DSPs including **Dis-Chem Courier**. [https://www.fedhealth.co.za/member-support/obtaining-chronic-medication/] (A, n.d.)
- **Remedi:** "DisChem Pharmacies are designated service providers for chronic medicines only." [https://www.yourremedi.co.za/schemes/remedi/designated-service-providers] (A, n.d.)
- **De Beers Benefit Society** has a Dis-Chem page (arrangement details not captured). [https://dbbs.co.za/dis-chem/] (A, n.d.)
- Bonitas, Medshield, Momentum: **no Dis-Chem-specific DSP evidence found**.

**Facts: channels**
- **Dis-Chem Direct / courier:** scripts can be emailed to documents@dischem.co.za with medical aid name, option, membership number and main-member flag. Teams handle "monthly medication reminders, medical aid authorisations and delivery status enquiries". [https://www.dischem.co.za/my-services/dispensary] [https://www.dischem.co.za/repeat-prescriptions] (A, n.d.)
- **Dis-Chem Online does not claim from medical aid:** "Dis-Chem Online currently does not process Medical Aid claims. You can submit your claim to your scheme for reimbursement as per scheme rules." [https://www.dischem.co.za/site-terms-and-conditions] / [https://www.dischem.co.za/site-faqs] (A, n.d.)
- **Pack My Meds** and the app let customers upload new or repeat scripts for pre-packing and collection. [https://www.dischem.co.za/pack-my-meds] (A)
- **Healthbridge e-scripting:** a free feature in Healthbridge Clinical lets doctors "send secure, paperless prescriptions directly to any Dis-Chem pharmacy". [https://healthbridge.co.za/seamless-prescribing-with-healthbridge-dis-chem/] (C-vendor, but a first-party partner announcement)
- **Seasonality:** January retail recovery "largely driven by the availability of medical aid benefits" (CEO Rui Morais). [https://dailyinvestor.com/retail/79029/dis-chem-medical-aid-win/] (B, 2025-02-21)

**Facts: clinics**
- **Clinic Connect (Dis-Chem Wellness Clinics):** nurse consult with history and exam **R99**; if needed, a video consult with a doctor, including extra tests, **R275**. "Medical aid rates may differ from cash rates." Call centre 086 111 7427. [https://www.dischem.co.za/clinic-connect] (A, n.d.). An older Dis-Chem Facebook post quoted R90 / R257 [https://www.facebook.com/Dischem/photos/a.10150234033310794/10158247905560794/] (A-social, older), so prices have risen.
- Aggregator sites put doctor consults at Dis-Chem clinics at "R400 to R550" (C, not reliable) [https://clinicpricelist.com/dischem-clinic-price-list/].
- **Health Hub** (Melrose Arch, 323rd outlet, May 2026) combines clinic, pharmacy and "cover services": virtual GP, diagnostics, digital prescriptions, healthcare advisers. [https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-05-08-dis-chem-bets-on-integrated-health-hubs-to-tackle-fragmented-healthcare-system/] (B, 2026-05-08) [https://it-online.co.za/2026/05/07/dis-chem-debuts-new-healthcare-concept/] (C, 2026-05-07)
- The telemedicine / clinic software came through the **Healthforce** acquisition (acquisitions circular covering Baby City, Medicare, Healthforce and Kaelo). [https://thevault.exchange/?get_group_doc=6262%2F1610090289-Acquisitions-BabyCityMedicareHealthforceKaelo.pdf] (A, ~Jan 2021)

**Not found**
- **Which claims switch(es) Dis-Chem uses** (MediKredit HealthNet ST / SwitchOn / Healthbridge). No public statement. The dispensing system is Unisolv lineage (see section 5), and Unisolv-type systems usually route through MediKredit, but that is **not confirmed**.

## 4. Dis-Chem insurance and financial products

**Facts**
- **Dis-Chem Health (medical insurance and gap cover):** insurer **Centriq Insurance Company Limited** (FSP 3417, non-life); administrator **Kaelo Risk (Pty) Ltd** (FSP 36931). [https://dischemhealth.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Dis-Chem-Health-2022-Understanding-your-Plus-Policy-Cover_V9.pdf] [https://dischemhealth.co.za/gap-policy-pack-2024/DCH-Gap-Cover-2024-Base-Brochure.pdf] (A)
- **Plans and 2026 prices:** MyHealth Core **R549 pm** main member / R375 child; MyHealth Plus **R765 pm** / R492 child (aggregator figures) [https://medicalaid.com/health-insurance/dischem/] (C); "MyHealth Plus starts from R765 per month" [https://dischemhealth.co.za/medical-insurance/myhealth-plus/] (A). Accident cover and a "Vital" plan also exist (names from the CMS ruling coverage).
- **Benefits:** unlimited GP visits and virtual consults on the **Prime Cure network**; **8 visits a year to a network pharmacy clinic**, with nurse visits "in a Dis-Chem **or Clicks** clinic". [https://dischemhealth.co.za/medical-insurance/myhealth-plus/] (A)
- **Claims at point of care:** "You shouldn't need to claim … provided you are using Network Providers, claims are paid on your behalf … present your digital membership card via the Dis-Chem Health App along with your ID". [https://dischemhealth.co.za/faq/] (A)
- **Premium collection is by debit order:** "All Premiums are payable monthly in advance, on your chosen debit order strike date … 30-day grace period after which we will automatically deduct the arrear Premiums (i.e. do a double deduction)"; "Should you cancel or stop your debit order, it will be deemed that you have cancelled your cover". [https://dischemhealth.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Dis-Chem-Gap-2023-Base-Policy.pdf] (A)
- **Refunds / claims admin** go to dischemhealth@kaelo.co.za with a refund form, ID, bank details, practice number, tariff codes and receipt. [https://dischemhealth.co.za/info-hub/medical-insurance/navigating-the-claims-process-how-to-claim-for-medical-insurance/] (A)
- **Regulatory risk (CMS ruling):** the CMS directed Centriq in 2024 to stop the white-label arrangement. The Appeals Board dismissed Centriq's appeal over Dec 2025/Jan 2026, holding that "consumer perception is decisive" and that Centriq should have sought approval before using the "Dis-Chem MyHealth Core / Vital" branding. Dis-Chem called it "a clarification of process". [https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-01-26-dis-chem-defends-health-insurance-partnership-after-cms-branding-ruling/] (B, 2026-01-26) [https://www.moonstone.co.za/appeals-board-ruling-puts-centriq-dis-chem-partnership-on-the-line/] (B) [https://www.medicalbrief.co.za/cms-rules-on-healthcare-products-branding-in-dis-chem-dispute/] (B)
- **Ownership:** Dis-Chem took **25% of Kaelo Holdings** (Nov 2021, R160m, up to R195m) [https://www.listcorp.com/jse/dcp/dis-chem-pharmacies-limited/news/trading-statement-and-voluntary-announcement-relating-to-kaelo-acquisition-2612678.html] (A, 2021). It bought **50% of OneSpark** (life insurance, R155.9m, 2024) [https://www.moonstone.co.za/dis-chem-agrees-to-buy-50-stake-in-insurer-onespark/] (B, 2024).
- **Dis-Chem Life** (FSP 50594): life, critical illness, **chronic life cover**, funeral and education cover. Funeral is underwritten by **Guardrisk Life Limited** (FSP 76): "up to R125,000 funeral cover from just R69 per month … up to 21 family members", claims paid in 4–48 hours. [https://www.dischemlife.co.za/offer/funeral-cover/] (A, n.d.) Launch coverage: [https://www.cover.co.za/news/dis-chem-make-life-insurance-more-accessible-and-affordable] (B, 2025-03-05)
- **Staff clinic:** first on-site employee clinic at the Midrand head office / DC, serving 2,500+ staff, with plans for more DCs. [https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/dis-chem-redefines-workplace-wellness-with-first-on-site-employee-clinic-175926a] [https://lifestyleandtech.co.za/company-news/article/2026-07-15/dis-chem-opens-first-on-site-staff-clinic-as-employers-rethink-workplace-healthcare] (B/C, 2026-07-15)

**Not found**
- Whether premiums can be **paid or policies sold in store** (the Health Hub "cover services" desk suggests in-store sales; payment method unknown).
- Hospital cash product: not found.
- How Dis-Chem Health network claims at Dis-Chem clinics are settled between Kaelo and Dis-Chem (a receivable from Kaelo / Centriq?).
- Policy / lives count: not public in the results found.

## 5. Financial reporting clues

**Facts**

[https://www.insidentity.com/dis-chem-pharmacies-audited-annual-consolidated-results-for-the-twelve-months-ended-28-february-2026/] (B, ~2026-05-29) [https://supermarket.co.za/index.php/retailer-trading-results/7949-…] (B) [https://dailyinvestor.com/retail/136483/r5-billion-in-five-years-say-goodbye-to-dis-chem-as-you-know-it/] (B, ~2026-06)
- **SENS results announcement** 29 May 2026 [https://www.sharenet.co.za/v3/sens_display.php?tdate=20260529070500&seq=3&scode=] (A). **Sustainability Report FY2026** [https://dischemgroup.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/DIS-CHEM-SR-2026-INTERACTIVE.pdf] (A, Jul 2026). I could not read either.
- **Trade and other receivables R3.57bn at 31 Aug 2025** (R3,568,496k) per the HY2026 interim results. [https://senspdf.jse.co.za/documents/2025/jse/isse/dcpe/HY26.pdf] (A, Oct 2025; figure from search snippet)
- **Head-office restructure:** a Section 189 process covers 500+ head-office roles (≤2.4% of staff); 200 new head-office roles go to "areas … historically underinvested"; stores and DCs are unaffected; it follows "eight months of planning for a new operating model". [https://www.news24.com/business/companies/dis-chem-retrenchments-500-head-office-staff-in-consultations-20260424-1045] (B, 2026-04-24) [https://iol.co.za/business/jobs/2026-04-25-dis-chems-corporate-restructuring-500-jobs-under-review-and-new-roles-created/] (B, 2026-04-25)

SAP All-in-One went live in 2009 across finance, merchandising, WMS and BW via UCS Solutions [https://cisp.cachefly.net/assets/articles/attachments/46613_sap_and_ucs_solutions_play_a_role_in_dis-chem%E2%80%99s_phenomenal_growth.pdf] (C, old).
