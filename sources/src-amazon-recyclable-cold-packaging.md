## Summary

Amazon's answer to cold-chain packaging waste at consumer scale was not a
returnable box but a kerbside-recyclable paper insulation liner for Amazon
Fresh and Whole Foods deliveries, replacing plastic film, cotton fibre and
mixed-plastic bubble insulation. Amazon Pharmacy's refrigerated shipments are
described separately as temperature-protective packaging; whether they use the
same paper liner is not stated. Direct fetch blocked on 2026-09-07; claims from
search extracts of Grocery Dive and About Amazon.

## Key claims

- 2021: Amazon launched curbside-recyclable insulated packaging made from
  recycled paper for chilled and frozen grocery delivery in the US.
- Amazon states the switch replaces about 735,000 lb of plastic film, 3.15
  million lb of natural cotton fibre and 15 million lb of non-recyclable mixed
  plastic per year (company figures).
- The liner uses layered recycled paper tissue and removes the need for plastic
  liners or bubble insulation; produced regionally in the US.
- Temperature-sensitive same-day grocery items are delivered in insulated bags
  recyclable in most US kerbside programmes (2025 press release).
- Amazon Pharmacy: refrigerated medication ships separately in packaging
  designed to keep a safe temperature; no take-back described.
