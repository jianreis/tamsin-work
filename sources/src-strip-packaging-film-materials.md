## Summary

Vendor material describing what the film in automated dose-sachet packagers
(Parata PASS/ATP, Synmed, RxSafe RapidPak, Yuyama) is made of: rolls sold as
paper, foil or cellophane, with the common laminates being polyethylene bonded
to cellophane, polyester or foil. Laminates cannot go through ordinary film
recycling; mono-material (all-PE or all-PP) films can, and cellophane is
cellulose-based. Vendor claims, so tier C; useful to state the material
question honestly, not to assert recyclability of any specific machine's roll.
Direct fetch blocked on 2026-09-07; claims from search extracts.

## Key claims

- Pharmacy automation suppliers sell "paper/foil/cellophane rolls" and custom
  dispensing boxes for Parata PASS, Parata ATP, Synmed Hybrid, RxSafe RapidPak
  and Yuyama Litrea packagers.
- Strip packaging films are typically aluminium foil, polyethylene, cellophane,
  polyester or laminates of these (polyethylene-cellophane,
  polypropylene-cellophane-polyethylene, polycarbonate-polyethylene).
- Multi-layer laminates give barrier protection but are not recyclable in
  standard flexible-film streams; all-PE or all-PP mono-material films are
  compatible with store drop-off film recycling in the US.
- Cellophane is described as the most common cellulose-based biopolymer
  advancing toward biodegradable packaging film (general claim, not
  pharmacy-specific).
- Amazon/PillPack states its packets are PET (see [[src-pillpack-packaging-help]]).
