## Summary

The one documented case of a retail pharmacy shipping cold-chain prescriptions
to patients in a returnable, reconditioned insulated box. Walmart Specialty
Pharmacy used Entropy Solutions' Greenbox: an HDPE outer with reusable
insulation panels, holding 2 to 8 degrees C for four days, reclaimed and cleaned
at Entropy facilities and shipped back for reuse, about 20 cycles guaranteed and
an average of 55 observed in carrier circulation. Walmart reported a 50%
shipping-cost saving. Trade-press account based on vendor claims, dated (c.
2013; Greenbox was later sold to ThermoSafe), so tier C. Direct fetch blocked on
2026-09-07; claims from search extracts.

## Key claims

- Greenbox: tinted reusable HDPE outer shipping container with reusable or
  recyclable Thermal-Lok insulation panels (stated R-value 40).
- Holds a cold-chain prescription at temperature for four days; ambient for
  more than five days.
- Reuse: as many as 20 cycles via reclamation at three North American and two
  European facilities that inspect and clean boxes; tracking showed an average
  of 55 uses in FedEx/UPS circulation, some customers up to 100.
- Walmart Specialty Pharmacy reported 50% shipping-cost savings.
- Return leg relied on the parcel carrier network, not a pharmacy driver.
- Entropy Solutions later sold the Greenbox line to ThermoSafe Brands
  (Healthcare Packaging press release).
