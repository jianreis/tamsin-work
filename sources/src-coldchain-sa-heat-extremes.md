## Summary

The climate envelope any outdoor locker has to survive. Three South African
stations already average 59 to 83 days a year above 35°C, and projections under
RCP4.5 push that past 100 days in the mid-future. Upington's record is 45.3°C.
Pretoria's average maximum for the hottest month is 29°C, with a heatwave threshold
at 34°C.

Set against the USP controlled-room-temperature ceiling of 25°C mean kinetic
temperature and a 30°C brief-excursion limit, this is the problem statement. In
parts of South Africa, outdoor ambient alone sits above the excursion limit for
months of the year, before any solar gain on a steel enclosure is added.

## Key claims

- Current annual average frequency of daily maximum above 35°C: **Vanwyksvlei 59
  days, Musina 78 days, Upington 83 days**.
- Under RCP4.5, all three are projected to exceed **100 days a year above 35°C** in
  the mid-future.
- **Upington record maximum: 45.3°C, January 2016** (station record 1949 to June 2025).
- **Pretoria** average maximum for the hottest month is **29°C**, heatwave threshold
  **34°C**.
- SAWS declares a heatwave when maximum temperatures exceed the average for the
  hottest month by 5°C or more for three or more consecutive days.
- National average annual temperature about 26.1°C with over 3,150 hours of sunshine
  a year.
