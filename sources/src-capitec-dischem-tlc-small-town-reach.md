## Summary

This is the fact that complicates the premise of a bank-branch collection point.
"No Dis-Chem in that town" is true of the 316 corporate retail pharmacies, but
Dis-Chem already reaches small towns through The Local Choice franchise, which
grew from 240 to 280 stores in the year to May 2026, and through a wholesale
relationship with more than 1,000 independent pharmacies, many of them in small
and rural towns. Management sees room for 500 TLC stores. Before treating a
Capitec branch as the only viable node in a small town, the concept has to
answer why a TLC franchise or serviced independent in the same town is not the
better collection point, given it is already a licensed pharmacy with a
pharmacist on the premises.

## Key claims

- **The Local Choice (TLC) franchise grew from 240 to 280 stores** in the
  reporting period to May 2026, a **16.4%** increase.
- Dis-Chem management sees room for the independent pharmacy platform to reach
  **500 stores**.
- Dis-Chem **services more than 1,000 independent pharmacies**, which have the
  option to convert to TLC.
- **Many of those independents are in small and rural towns**, which is the
  stated strategic point of the platform.
- Dis-Chem reported **316 retail pharmacy stores and 42 retail baby stores** at
  28 February 2026, having opened or acquired **31 retail pharmacy stores** in
  FY2026, and targets **34 new stores** in the year ahead.
- **No public store-by-town list** was found for either Dis-Chem or TLC, so the
  overlap with Capitec's town coverage cannot be computed from disclosures.
