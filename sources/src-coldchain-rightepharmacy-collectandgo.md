## Summary

The most instructive South African precedent, because it shows what a compliant
medicine locker actually costs in engineering terms. Collect&Go lockers are not
passive steel boxes. Each unit carries a side-mounted air-conditioning unit and a
remote temperature monitor reporting into the operator's cloud, and the vendor
states the lockers were customised to meet pharmacy regulation. They need a 220V
mains supply, though not data connectivity, which is what lets them go into remote
sites.

So the industry answer to "can an unattended locker hold storage conditions" is
already yes, for **ambient** storage, with active conditioning and telemetry. That
is a different claim from holding 2-8°C. Nothing in the published material
describes a refrigerated compartment or cold-chain items in Collect&Go, and the
service is built to support CCMDD, which excludes insulin.

Reliability C: these are vendor and trade-association claims, self-reported, with
no published temperature-performance data.

## Key claims

- Collect&Go lockers store **pre-dispensed medicine parcels** for patient
  self-collection; introduced 2020 by Right ePharmacy.
- Lockers are **temperature controlled with a built-in side-system air-conditioning
  unit**, monitored through data collected by the eRx Cloud solution.
- Lockers are **customised to meet stringent pharmacy regulations** (vendor claim,
  regulation not named).
- Lockers require only a **220V power source** and **do not need data connectivity**,
  which enables installation in remote and rural locations.
- Full parcel tracking via a live dashboard; uncollected medicine is flagged and
  followed up.
- Patients must be evaluated and declared clinically stable by a clinician before
  enrolment.
- Scale: **335,544 chronic prescriptions** dispensed through the lockers within two
  years of launch; **39 smart locker sites** announced for Gauteng; further sites in
  Free State; interest from Botswana, Eswatini and Lesotho.
- Supports the NDoH last-mile dispensing and CCMDD programmes and the national
  adherence strategy.
- **No refrigerated compartment or cold-chain product line is described** in the
  published material. `⚠️ UNVERIFIED` - absence in vendor material, not a stated
  exclusion.
