## Summary

Capitec's stated minimum branch hours run Monday to Friday 08:00-17:00,
Saturday 08:00-13:00, and Sunday 09:00-13:00 at branches that open on Sundays.
Mall branches trade materially longer, several to 18:00-20:00 on weekdays and
Saturdays and 17:00-19:00 on Sundays. That is a wider collection window than a
standalone pharmacy dispensary in a small town, and the Sunday cover is the part
worth testing, because it is branch-specific rather than universal.

Retrieval note: reliability is C, not B. The minimum-hours figure comes from a
reply on Capitec's official X account in 2023 rather than a formal disclosure,
and the mall hours come from shopping-centre tenant directories, which go stale.
The authoritative per-branch hours live in Capitec's branch locator
(`https://www.capitecbank.co.za/branch-locator/`), which this session could not
reach. Any concept that depends on a specific branch's Sunday hours needs those
checked branch by branch.

## Key claims

- Capitec's stated **minimum** branch hours: **Mon-Fri 08:00-17:00,
  Sat 08:00-13:00, Sun 09:00-13:00**.
- **Sunday opening is not universal** - "certain branches are open on Sundays".
- Capitec states there are **ATMs at all of its branches**, so an ATM is
  available outside branch hours even where the counter is closed.
- Mall-branch examples showing longer trading (retrieved 2026-08-16, from centre
  directories):
  - Boardwalk Mall (Gqeberha): Mon-Sat 09:00-19:00, Sun and public holidays 09:00-17:00
  - Fourways Mall (Johannesburg): Mon-Sat 09:00-20:00, Sun and public holidays 09:00-19:00
  - Hemingways Mall (East London): Mon-Sat 09:00-18:00, Sun and public holidays 09:00-17:00
  - Preller Square (Bloemfontein): Mon-Fri 09:00-18:00, Sat 09:00-17:00, Sun 10:00-14:00
  - Vangate Mall (Cape Town): Saturdays 08:00-13:00
- No source was found stating **what proportion of the 885 branches open on
  Sundays**, or the hours profile of non-mall and rural branches. This is an open
  gap.
