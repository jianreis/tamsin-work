# A deployed South African locker network holding dispensed scheduled medicine

## Summary

Right ePharmacy, the pharmacy arm of Right to Care, runs Collect&Go smart
lockers holding **pre-dispensed** medicine parcels for CCMDD patients, with 39
locker sites reported in Gauteng. The design details that matter here: the
lockers are described as **customised to meet stringent pharmacy regulations**,
with air-conditioning and remote temperature monitoring, which is a claim that
somebody worked the compliance question and concluded storage conditions were
the binding constraint. Eligibility is gated clinically before a patient is ever
enrolled: a clinician evaluates the patient, declares them stable, enrols them,
writes the script and sends it to the service provider, with a check that the
patient has been stable for at least 12 months and falls inside CCMDD criteria.

No pharmacist is present at the locker. The vendor makes no claim about
counselling at collection.

Treat this as a vendor source. It establishes that the model is deployed and
that its operators believe it is compliant. It is not evidence of what the
regulator has decided, and the words "customised to meet stringent pharmacy
regulations" are marketing copy, not a determination.

## Key claims

- Collect&Go lockers are used to **safely store pre-dispensed medicine parcels
  and to facilitate parcel collection by patients**. Dispensing happens
  elsewhere, at the service provider pharmacy.
- Lockers are **customised to meet pharmacy regulations** and include
  air-conditioning units and remote temperature monitors for storage conditions.
- Deployment reported at **39 smart locker sites in Gauteng**, supporting NDoH
  last-mile dispensing, the CCMDD programme and the national adherence strategy.
- Eligibility: a clinician evaluates and declares the patient **clinically
  stable**, enrols them, writes the script and sends it to the service provider.
  Criteria include stability for at least **12 months** and falling within CCMDD
  eligibility.
- **No pharmacist is present at the locker.** No counselling mechanism at the
  point of collection is described.
- The deployment sits inside the **public sector** CCMDD programme. No private
  retail equivalent was found.
