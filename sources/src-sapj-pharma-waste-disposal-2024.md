## Summary

A December 2024 practice guide for SA community pharmacies on handling
pharmaceutical waste in line with SAPC GPP rules and municipal by-laws. Its
premise is telling: the author found no readily available practice guidance and
existing SOPs insufficient, and built the recommendations from Western Cape
practice, including costs of waste-collection containers. It confirms that SA
has no national consumer take-back scheme comparable to Australia's RUM. Direct
fetch blocked on 2026-09-07; only the abstract-level extract was available, so
tier B and limited claims.

## Key claims

- Published SAPJ Vol 91 No 6, December 2024.
- Purpose: help community pharmacies manage pharmaceutical waste disposal in
  accordance with SAPC guidance, GPP rules and municipal by-laws.
- Author states useful practice guidelines on pharmaceutical waste disposal were
  not readily available and current SOPs were insufficient.
- Developed from practice settings in the Western Cape; includes costs of
  pharmaceutical waste collection containers.
- Related SA research (Kusturica-type household studies, Johannesburg 2022)
  finds patients have little knowledge of correct disposal and calls for
  take-back programmes at health facilities.
