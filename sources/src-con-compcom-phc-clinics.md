## Summary

Competition Commission working paper on private primary-healthcare clinics.
Sizes the private-PHC channel: up to ~28% of the population accesses private
PHC services, with a meaningful low-income, uninsured segment paying
out-of-pocket. Gives the going rate for private GP consultations. Together
with [[src-con-private-gp-uninsured]], establishes the "local private doctor"
as a mainstream first contact for the uninsured - the realistic origin of a
cash paper script that lands at retail.

fetch on 2026-09-06. Re-verify figures against the PDF.

## Key claims

- **Up to ~28% of the South African population accesses private PHC
  services**, including a meaningful segment of low-income, uninsured people
  paying out-of-pocket.
- **Private GP clinic consultations charge roughly R300-R750** (2023).
- Low-cost private clinic formats (including retail-adjacent nurse-led
  clinics) grew specifically to serve the uninsured cash market.
