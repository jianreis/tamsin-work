## Summary

The SAPC rules that govern what a pharmacy does with medicine that comes back
and with the containers it dispenses in. BN 100 of 2020 sets the GPP standard
for disposal and destruction of medicines: every item disposed of must be
recorded in the stock system, Schedule 5 and 6 quantities go into the register
with witnesses, and destruction goes through a waste facility authorised under
NEMWA. The consolidated GPP Manual carries sections titled "Dispensing
containers" and "Re-use of containers", but the text of those sections could
not be read. Direct fetch of every SAPC, SAFLII, UCT and gov.za copy was
blocked on 2026-09-07; claims are limited to search extracts, and the specific
wording on patient-returned medicine and container re-use is marked unverified.

## Key claims

- BN 100 of 2020 amends the GPP rules with a standard on disposal and
  destruction of medicines and scheduled substances.
- All medicines and scheduled substances for disposal must be recorded in a
  pharmacy stock management system.
- For specified Schedule 5 and Schedule 6 substances, quantities to be disposed
  of must be entered in the relevant register and signed by the required
  witnesses.
- Destruction may be carried out by a waste treatment facility authorised under
  the National Environmental Management: Waste Act 59 of 2008.
- The consolidated GPP Manual contains sections headed "Dispensing containers"
  and "Re-use of containers" (table of contents, page reference c. 67). Their
  wording, and whether re-use of a patient's container is permitted under
  conditions or prohibited, is ⚠️ UNVERIFIED pending a read of the PDF.
- No SAPC rule text explicitly stating "returned medicine may not be
  re-dispensed" was retrievable; the inference rests on the disposal standard
  (returns enter the disposal pathway), on SAHPRA destruction guidance for
  undesirable products, and on the identical rule in comparable jurisdictions
  ([[src-rum-natrum-australia]], [[src-nabp-return-reuse-position]]). Marked
  ⚠️ UNVERIFIED as a direct SA citation.
