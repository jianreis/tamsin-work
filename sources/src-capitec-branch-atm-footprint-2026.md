## Summary

Capitec runs the largest bank branch network in South Africa and is still adding
to it while the big four shrink theirs. At the February 2026 year end the bank
reported 885 branches and 8,771 branded ATMs, spread across suburbs, townships
and rural areas. The bank told MyBroadband it planned a net increase in both
networks during 2026. For a collection-point concept this is the core footprint
fact: Capitec has roughly 2.8 times as many physical sites as Dis-Chem has
retail pharmacies, and its stated reason for keeping them is reach into places
digital channels do not serve well.

## Key claims

- Capitec reported **885 branches** at the financial year ended **28 February 2026**,
  up from **853 in 2022**. This is described as the largest branch network in
  South Africa.
- The ATM network reached **8,771 machines** at FY2026, having grown by roughly
  **1,600 units since 2022**.
- At the time of the bank's February 2026 statement it described itself as
  operating **more than 860 branches and 8,500 branded ATMs** nationwide (a
  slightly earlier cut of the same numbers).
- Capitec stated it planned a **net increase in both branch footprint and ATM
  network during 2026**, against an industry trend of branch and ATM reduction.
- The branch network is described as covering **suburbs, townships and rural
  areas**, not only metros.
- Capitec said it would keep **relocating selected branches and ATMs** as market
  dynamics change, so specific sites are not permanent.
- No public disclosure was found giving a **branch count per province, per town,
  or split metro / non-metro**. That breakdown is not in the material reachable
  from this session.
