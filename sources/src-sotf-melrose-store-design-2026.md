# Summary

TDC&Co's 37-page 3D design pack for the Melrose Arch store (19 Feb 2026):
shopfront, perspective/axonometric plans, and room-level renders. This is the
physical form the SteerCo decisions took. Reliability A as the design record;
renders show design intent, not the as-built store.

# Key claims

- Shopfront: open double entrance, "Better health starts here" messaging, the
  four offer pillars signposted at entry (Clinic / Pharmacy / Cover / Better
  Rewards), Better Rewards launch banners ("Save 10% instantly … on 140+ of
  SA's most-loved brands"), Dis-Chem Life panel, gold cross-motif wall pattern.
- Layout matches the "T": back wall carries (left to right) Cover rooms,
  curved Pharmacy counter run, Clinic suite; the circular Healthcare Hub desk
  sits at the centre under a double gold "Healthcare" halo ring, with yellow
  stool seating around it; front shop flows either side; POS bank front-right
  with a customer-calling rail above.
- Pharmacy: long curved counter with divider screens between service points,
  wood-and-yellow palette, "Get to know your pharmacists better" board, big
  Better Rewards screen behind the counter; dispensary/collection counters
  face the clinic entrance (clinic script referrals walk straight across).
- Clinic: own signed entrance beside the pharmacy arch; waiting nook with
  planters and bench; pre-consult and consultation rooms with tiled splash
  walls, exam bed, "Here to help you feel better" / "Thoughtful care. Better
  health." wall copy; dedicated patient toilet.
- Cover: two advisor rooms with planted screens and wood panelling, semi-open
  curved reception edge onto the floor.
- Wellbeing Pod ("Wellbeing" drum) and Health & Fitness / Lifestyle Food
  category zones in front shop; brand-name skincare wall bays ("Essence by
  Brand", "Catrice by Brand" floor decals); category wall lexicon lines
  ("Self-care builds confidence", "Every day brings something new").
- Employee spaces designed to the priority-customer decisions: canteen with
  long table and kitchenette, manager office, gold cross motif carried into
  staff areas.
