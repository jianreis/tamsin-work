## Summary

Filed to answer a specific question and to record that it cannot be fully answered from public disclosure.

The national totals are clear. For the twelve months to 28 February 2026 Dis-Chem reported 316 retail pharmacy stores, having added 31 pharmacy outlets in the year, plus 42 baby stores. Clicks reported 1,003 Clicks stores with 797 pharmacies and 226 primary care clinics as at 28 February 2026, and has signalled a target of 1,200 stores.

Neither group discloses its store count by province in its results announcements, integrated annual report or investor presentations, at least not in any document retrievable in this session. The provincial split of the two chains that dominate corporate retail pharmacy in South Africa is therefore not publicly quantified. Anyone needing it has to derive it by scraping the store locators, which is a data-collection task rather than a research one, and which should be logged as such.

What the totals already establish: 316 plus 797 gives roughly 1,113 chain pharmacies, which tracks closely against the 1,110 corporate chain community pharmacies the 2023 peer-reviewed count reports ([[src-access-sa-community-pharmacy-sector-2023]]) and confirms that the two listed groups effectively are the corporate chain estate. That estate is described in the same paper as sited in cities and towns.

## Key claims

- Dis-Chem reported 316 retail pharmacy stores for the twelve months ended 28 February 2026, having opened 31 new pharmacy outlets in the period.
- Dis-Chem also operated 42 baby stores, having closed three Baby City stores in the period.
- Dis-Chem has stated a target of 34 new stores for the following year.
- Clicks reported 1,003 Clicks stores as at 28 February 2026, comprising 797 pharmacies and 226 primary care clinics.
- Clicks has signalled a store target of 1,200.
- Combined chain pharmacy count is approximately 1,113, close to the 1,110 corporate chain community pharmacies recorded in 2021 SAPC data.
- Neither Dis-Chem nor Clicks publicly discloses store or pharmacy counts by province. This figure is not available from company disclosure and would have to be derived from store locators.
