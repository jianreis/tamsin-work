# Pharmacy Script Journey IDIs — raw transcripts (9 of 10)

## Summary

The **raw auto-transcribed recordings behind
[[src-dc-script-journey-interviews-2025]]** (10 remote 1-hour IDIs, fielded
20–25 Oct 2025), supplied by Tamsin on 2026-08-20 — nine of the ten
participants. The report note remains the citable synthesis; these transcripts
exist for two jobs Tamsin named: **verbatim mining** (exact customer language
for journey and deliverable work) and **persona grounding** (these are real
customers whose lives can anchor persona detail). Files live in
`sources/transcripts/` as `idi-2025-10-<name>.docx`.

**Handling notes.** Transcripts are Teams auto-transcription: names, places
and product names are frequently garbled ("this game"/"disc came" = Dis-Chem,
"clicks" = Clicks, "polls"/"pulls" = pills) — quote them as-is but gloss where
needed. **POPIA:** participants are identified by full name with personal
health detail; internal use only, anonymise in anything customer- or
executive-facing. Two files are incomplete: **Rekha** captures only the
5-minute pre-interview admin (the interview itself is in a separate recording
— ⚠️ ask for it), and **Tshediso** cuts off at ~13 minutes when his
connection froze (the session was rescheduled — ⚠️ the continuation, plus the
tenth participant's transcript, are outstanding).

**Reliability — B:** primary qualitative material, same basis as the report
note; individual quotes are one customer's account.

## Participant catalogue

### 1. Claire Leske — `idi-2025-10-claire.docx` (51m)
**Profile:** 36, Melkbosstrand (Cape Town), GM of a real-estate firm, single
mom of two teenage boarding-school boys (both on chronic asthma-listed meds;
one son under investigation for diabetes), her own hypertension now controlled
post gastric sleeve; proxy-collects for her pensioner mother (state clinic
patient, no medical aid). Discovery/Vitality; **medical-aid savings depleted
by April** — paying everything out of pocket, still claiming for tax.
**Behaviour:** splits across Clicks (closer, 30% Vitality cash-back),
Dis-Chem (range, separate script/OTC queues, stock reliability) and an
independent; monthly ~R7,000 meds spend; fierce specials/Black-Friday planner;
wants everything at one pharmacy but a year script is stuck at the
independent ("they don't allow you to transfer scripts"); never used an app —
didn't know Dis-Chem had one; a Clicks online sign-up died in a "waiting"
state with unanswered follow-up emails.
**Friction:** profile lookup failed completely at one branch ("they didn't
pick up any of my details… I even gave them my disc game card"); her mother's
surname mismatch stalled a collection; refill timing rule ("wait until
you've only got 2 days pulls left") collides with boarding-school logistics —
an Uber for the meds costs R250–300; hates queues "with a passion".
**Delight:** separate script/OTC queues; knowledgeable counter advice her
doctor later endorsed; front-shop till queue moves fast despite its length.
**Verbatims:**
> "I've never really had a stock problem at this game, whereas it clicks quite often." (~26:04)
> "They said they didn't pick me up at all in the system, which was very strange because I've been there plenty times… I even gave them my disc game card and they couldn't pick me up." (~22:10–22:35)
> "I want to get to the stage where just everything is at one pharmacy… but they don't allow you to transfer scripts once you've handed it in at a set pharmacy." (~36:44)
> "If I could whichever pharmacy I could come right with with online deliveries, that'll be my go to even if it costs me more money… because I flip and hate going in queues." (~28:41)
**Persona hint:** the multi-pharmacy household medicine manager — closest to
Nomvula (insured chronic multimorbid) with a live caregiver-proxy thread.

## Cross-cutting observations (for the verbatim/persona work)

- **The queue is the universal pain and the universal differentiator**: every
  participant self-sorts pharmacies by queue behaviour, and the
  prescriptions-only desk (Noluthando) is the single most-loved artefact in
  the set.
- **Profile portability failures recur unprompted** (Claire twice, her mother
  once) — cross-branch and cross-surname lookup is lived pain, not an
  internal-ops abstraction.
- **Money timing ≠ affordability**: payday-coupled refill trips (Tamsin
  October, Dylan) and depleted-savings cash payments (Claire) shape *when*
  medicine is fetched more than *whether*.
- **The pharmacist-as-advocate** (Nkosinathi's live GEMS calls, Mathapelo's
  unpushy generic swap) is the trust engine the future state must not
  automate away.
- **Digital trust is conditional and personal**: one scam (Tshediso) or one
  dead sign-up flow (Claire's Clicks "waiting" state) closes the channel for
  years; human-arranged delivery and WhatsApp preference are the workarounds
  customers invent.
