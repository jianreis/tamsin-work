## Summary

The Dis-Chem and Capitec relationship starts in August 2021, not in 2025. Capitec
added Dis-Chem and Baby City to its Live Better rewards programme, paying
registered clients up to 2% cash back on selected items when they paid with a
Capitec card, in store or online. The cash back lands monthly in the client's
free Live Better savings account. Five years of a working card-linked
partnership is the relevant fact for a collection-point concept: the two parties
already have a commercial contract, a shared customer identifier at the point of
payment, and a track record of settling money between them.

## Key claims

- Capitec launched the **Dis-Chem and Baby City cash-back benefit in August 2021**.
- Benefit: **up to 2% cash back on selected items**, in store and on the
  Dis-Chem and Baby City websites.
- Conditions: the client must be **registered for Live Better** and must **pay
  with a Capitec card (debit or credit) or scan to pay** where available.
- Cash back is **paid monthly into the client's free Capitec Live Better savings
  account**.
- The **Live Better savings account launched mid-2021** and is described by
  Capitec as a behavioural banking product; joining is free and the benefits are
  the same regardless of income.
- Capitec publishes formal **partnership rules** for the Dis-Chem arrangement,
  which means the relationship is contractual and documented, not a marketing
  tie-up.
