# Summary

The 5-page decision log issued after SteerCo #1 (12 Nov 2025): per store area,
what was decided versus what remained open. This is the cleanest record of the
SOTF design decisions as ratified by business leadership. Reliability A —
company primary record of its own decisions.

# Key claims (decisions made, per area)

## Dispensary
- Pharmacy at the back of the store; decoupled submission and pickup (separate
  counters); multiple submission options (counter with pharmacist, in-store
  digital screen, app/WhatsApp); consultation possible at drop-off or pickup;
  unified OTC + script process (no separate OTC counter); optimised
  back-of-house pick-and-pack; queue removal via digital ticketing end-to-end.
- Left open: screen locations and functionality; lockers (dispensary/OTC only,
  not front shop); payment at the dispensary counter.

## Clinic
- Nurse-led diagnostics and primary care; triage room staffed by auxiliary
  nurse (nurse at top of license); dedicated clinic waiting room and patient
  toilet; admin (welcome, consent, booking) reallocated to a receptionist at
  the Hub; integrated script sharing clinic→dispensary; patient information
  shared across the ecosystem.
- Left open: clinic point of sale (Hub vs checkout); phase-2 micro-services
  and pricing.

## Cover
- Financial advisor always available in a dedicated cover room; awareness
  triggers across the store; booking system (no ticketing); bi-directional
  clinic↔cover flow.

## Hub
- Central hub in all stores bringing the ecosystem together: Better Rewards
  sign-up, reception for cover and clinic, traffic direction; multi-skilled
  staff at all times; integrated dispensary waiting area; gamified Better
  Rewards elements.

## Front shop, innovation aisle, retail innovations
- Dedicated category experiences for captainship categories: health bar
  (vitamin-deficiency testing, sampling, personalised supplement quiz), skin
  care (AR skin assessment as lead experience, "Trending in skin care", AR
  cosmetics via supplier partnerships e.g. L'Oreal/Maybelline),
  grooming/shaving touch-and-feel with strategic suppliers.
- Innovation (drive) aisle reserved year-round for Platinum vendors / Better
  Rewards offerings; seasonal display separate.
- Express checkout; digital catalogue; in-aisle navigation; promo push
  notifications. **Front-shop click & collect explicitly not implemented
  "until customer need is validated during relaunch of e-commerce"** — the
  one decision in the log conditioned on customer validation.

## Priority customer (employees) & operations
- BOH requirements: multi-purpose social space, multi-purpose meeting
  room/manager office, employee toilet, cash office, personalised lockers;
  training on the store floor using category experiences; camera/online/server
  rooms rationalised.
