## Summary

A deliberately weak note, filed because the comparison it supports matters and because no A-tier version of it could be retrieved in this session.

The comparison: South Africa has roughly 3,580 registered community pharmacies ([[src-access-sa-community-pharmacy-sector-2023]]) and roughly the same number of public primary health care clinics and community health centres, on the order of 3,500 to 3,700, plus about 400 public hospitals. Those two estates serve very different populations. Around 83 to 84% of South Africans have no medical scheme cover and depend on the public facilities; the retail pharmacy estate is sized to the insured minority and to cash customers in economically active areas.

That is the structural shape of the access gap in one line. It is also why Ward and colleagues found that all nine provinces met a 1-per-10,000 benchmark only once clinics were counted alongside pharmacies ([[src-access-ward-pharmacy-equity-2014]]). For most South Africans the clinic is the pharmacy.

Reliability is C and the note should be superseded. The facility counts here come from secondary summaries and a vendor blog surfaced in search, not from the National Department of Health District Health Barometer or the Health Systems Trust facility list, both of which would carry the figure at A tier and are blocked by this session's egress proxy. The uninsured share should be taken from the CMS annual industry report already in the vault ([[src-sch-cms-industry-report-2024]]) rather than from here. Do not quote the facility counts in a client deliverable without pulling the DHB or HST source first.

## Key claims

- South Africa has over 3,500 primary health care clinics and community health centres (secondary summary, unverified against a primary facility list).
- One secondary summary puts the public system at approximately 4,200 facilities: 403 hospitals and more than 3,700 community health centres and clinics.
- Public facilities serve an estimated 50 million people, the majority uninsured.
- Approximately 8.6 million people (about 17%) have private medical scheme cover, implying roughly 83% uninsured.
- Implication for the access gap: the community pharmacy estate (~3,580) and the public PHC estate (~3,500 to 3,700) are similar in count, but the pharmacy estate is sited to the insured and cash-paying minority while the clinic estate carries the uninsured majority.
