## Summary

Capitec branches still handle cash over the counter, but the bank prices that
channel to push cash away from the counter. Depositing notes at a branch costs
R4.00 per R100, nearly three times the R1.40 per R100 at a cash-accepting ATM,
and a withdrawal at a supermarket till costs R2.00 flat against R10.00 per
R1,000 at an ATM. This matters directly to the concept's cash-at-the-counter
release mechanic: a Capitec branch can take the money, but the bank's own
pricing says the counter is the channel it least wants used for cash, and any
partner arrangement would have to be a merchant-payment flow rather than a
customer depositing cash into an account.

## Key claims

- **Notes deposited at a branch: R4.00 per R100.**
- **Notes deposited at a cash-accepting ATM: R1.40 per R100.**
- **Coins (branch or self-service coin machine): R15.00 per R100.**
- **Cash withdrawal at any South African bank's ATM: R10.00 per R1,000.**
- **Cash withdrawal at a supermarket till point** (Pick n Pay, Shoprite,
  Checkers, Boxer): **R2.00** fixed.
- Business banking integrated cash solutions are priced far lower
  (R0.19-R0.21 per R100), which is the pricing tier a merchant collection
  arrangement would sit in rather than the personal deposit tier.
- Fee schedule stated as effective **1 March 2026**.
