# Summary

A one-page programme timeline (image, supplied by Tamsin on 2026-09-16)
showing the Store of the Future openings planned for the last quarter of
2026: 15 stores in three waves, six milestone gates per store (procurement,
delivery and staging, installation, configuration, testing and QA, store
opening), and three further stores on hold pending landlord handover.
Reliability A as the programme's own plan; the dates are plans, not
outcomes, and the plan itself notes that waves 2 and 3 overlap through
November with shared installation and staging crews.

# Key claims

- 15 SOTF stores are planned to open between 22 Oct and 3 Dec 2026, in
  three waves. Three more are on hold until landlord handover is released.
- Wave 1, October openings, 6 stores: Cluster A, Rand View (1 store,
  opening 22 Oct); Cluster B, "Oct 29 cluster" (5 stores, opening 29 Oct).
  Wave span 28 Sep to 29 Oct.
- Wave 2, November openings, 6 stores: Cluster C, Umhlanga Crescent
  (1 store, 1 Nov); Cluster D, "Nov 26 cluster" (5 stores, 26 Nov). Wave span
  8 Oct to 26 Nov.
- Wave 3, December openings, 3 stores: Cluster E, "Dec 03 cluster"
  (3 stores, 3 Dec). Wave span 9 Nov to 3 Dec.
- Each store runs six gates: M1 procurement, M2 delivery and staging,
  M3 installation, M4 configuration, M5 testing and QA, M6 store opening.
- Waves 2 and 3 overlap through November, so installation crews and staging
  capacity are shared across Clusters D and E in the same three weeks.
- Read with [[src-sotf-otc-workshop-2026]]: all of these stores open before
  the first ROWA (automated picker) store, planned for 2027, so all run the
  manual pick-and-pack process. The brainstorm's working figure of "~20 new
  stores before ROWA" is consistent with 15 planned plus 3 on hold plus early
  2027 openings.
