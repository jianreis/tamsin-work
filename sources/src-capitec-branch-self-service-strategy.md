## Summary

Capitec is rebuilding the branch around self-service hardware, and that is the
part of its strategy a collection concept would plug into. Self-service
terminals now handle more than 60% of branch interactions, five to six million
customer interactions a month, with the stated aim of freeing consultants for
complex work. The bank already runs a queue-management system that lets staff
approach waiting customers by stated need, and it already installs
government-linked self-service terminals for Smart ID. A dispensing locker or
handover counter would be another terminal in a branch that is being filled with
terminals anyway. The commercial reason to want it follows from Capitec's
earnings mix: fintech and insurance are 53% of group headline earnings, so a
monthly, date-certain reason for a client to walk into a branch has value beyond
the transaction itself.

## Key claims

- **Self-service terminals handle more than 60% of branch interactions**, about
  **5 million to 6 million customer interactions per month**.
- The stated purpose is to let terminals absorb simple tasks and **free
  consultants for complex issues**.
- Capitec runs **Qmatic queue management** in branches, described as letting
  staff engage waiting customers based on their stated service need.
- Third-party commentary describes Capitec branches as functioning as **"learning
  centres"** where consultants help clients use digital channels. This framing is
  secondary commentary, not sourced to Capitec. ⚠️ UNVERIFIED as a company
  statement.
- Commentary states Capitec operated **880 branches and 8,798 ATMs as at February
  2025**. Note the ATM figure sits slightly *above* the 8,771 reported for
  February 2026 in [[src-capitec-branch-atm-footprint-2026]]. One of the two is
  wrong, or the definitions differ (branded versus owned). Reconcile against the
  annual report before using either.
- Capitec is described as running a **hybrid channel strategy**: physical reach
  for clients with limited digital access, plus a push to digital adoption.
