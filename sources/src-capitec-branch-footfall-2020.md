## Summary

The only branch-footfall figure found for Capitec is six years old: about
7.8 million retail client visits a month across 852 branches in 2020, roughly
9,100 visits per branch per month, or around 350 a day. Use it as an order of
magnitude and nothing more. Capitec's app client base has grown from a fraction
of its clients to 15.3 million since then, and the bank's own commentary says
routine activity has been moving off the counter, so the 2020 figure is almost
certainly an overstatement of today's counter traffic. No current disclosure of
branch visits was found.

Retrieval note: techfinancials.co.za was blocked by this session's egress
policy; the figure comes from a search-index summary. Rated C on both age and
retrieval. If footfall matters to the business case, the number to ask Capitec
for is current monthly branch visits and average dwell time.

## Key claims

- **About 7.8 million retail client visits per month** across Capitec's
  **852 retail branches**, reported in 2020.
- Implied average of roughly **9,100 visits per branch per month** (derived, not
  stated in the source).
- Capitec described a shift toward clients starting onboarding on the app and
  **visiting a branch only to collect a physical card**.
- **No 2025 or 2026 branch-footfall disclosure was found** in the material
  reachable from this session.
