## Summary

The load-shedding assumption the vault has been carrying needs updating for 2026.
South Africa reached 300 consecutive days without load-shedding on 12 March 2026,
and the last cuts were a brief episode in mid-May 2025. Eskom's Summer Outlook for
1 September 2025 to 31 March 2026 forecast no load-shedding, and the Winter 2026
base case is no load-shedding through 31 August 2026.

The risk has not gone away, it has become conditional. Eskom's own high-risk
scenario, officially on the table between 18 May and 12 August 2026, resumes cuts
at stages 2 to 6 if unplanned breakdowns exceed 16,000 MW at winter peak. So a cold
chain design cannot assume daily outages any more, and it also cannot assume the
grid. It has to survive a return to stage 4-plus without notice.

Reliability B: Eskom statements are primary, but the consecutive-days figure and
the risk-window dates come via aggregator sites rather than the Eskom release
itself.

## Key claims

- **300 consecutive days without load-shedding as at 12 March 2026.**
- Last load-shedding was implemented **briefly in mid-May 2025**.
- Eskom **Summer Outlook (1 Sep 2025 - 31 Mar 2026): no load-shedding forecast**.
- Eskom **Winter Outlook 2026 base case: no load-shedding through 31 Aug 2026**.
- Generation recovery added about **4,000 MW**.
- **High-risk scenario**: if unplanned generation breakdowns exceed **16,000 MW** at
  2026 winter peak, cuts resume at **stages 2 to 6**. The risk window is officially
  **18 May to 12 August 2026**.
- Each stage represents roughly 1,000 MW shed; stage 1 corresponds to 0-2 hours
  without power per day, in blocks typically of two hours, sometimes four.
