## Summary

Reporting on the CCMDD programme's mechanics and trajectory. Confirms and
sharpens the collection cadence the vault already holds ([[src-con-ccmdd-ndoh]]:
parcels every 2-3 months): pre-packed medicine is delivered to pick-up points
on a roughly **two-month cycle**, patients usually receive **about three
months' supply**, and **from April 2026 stable patients move to six-month
supplies**. Kills any design assumption of a monthly programme parcel - the
gaps are long and getting longer, which raises the value of a service that
remembers the cycle for the household.

## Key claims

- Medication is **pre-packed centrally and delivered to pick-up points on a
  ~2-month cycle**; stable patients collect **every 2-3 months**.
- Patients usually receive **about three months' supply** per parcel.
- **From April 2026, stable patients enrolled at least three months get
  six-month supplies** - a cost-saving change that further lengthens the gap
  between collections.
- The PUP network has grown past **3,500 pick-up points**.
