## Summary

South African field data on how often cold chain actually breaks at the delivery
end. In a KwaZulu-Natal study of COVID-19 vaccine wastage, cold-chain failure
accounted for 2% of vials lost in the public sector and 15% in the private sector
during the period studied. The private-sector figure carries the most weight here,
because private facilities are the closer analogue to a retail pharmacy network:
more sites, smaller fridges, less centralised monitoring.

This is vaccine-specific and pandemic-specific, so it is not a general SA pharmacy
excursion rate. It does establish that cold-chain failure in South African
facilities is measurable and non-trivial, in buildings with staff, mains power and
purpose-built fridges. An unattended remote locker starts from a worse position on
all three.
