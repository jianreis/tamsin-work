# Summary

The strategy document behind the whole Better Health programme, of which Store
of the Future is the physical expression. 17 pages, August 2025, "CONFIDENTIAL
AND PROPRIETARY" consulting format. It states the SA healthcare problem, the
"Better Health Starts Here" vision, the ecosystem "flywheel", the target
customer segments, a "health universe" scoping exercise, and five building
blocks. Tamsin flags it as the reference frame for all Bigly/Dis-Chem work.

**Reliability A for what Dis-Chem intends and believes** (company primary
strategy). Claims about the SA market inside it (coverage splits, practitioner
ratios) are consulting-sourced and should be triangulated against the vault's
regulatory/schemes sources before reuse.

# Key claims

## Problem statement (SA healthcare)
- Five failures named: poor affordability (private cover unaffordable; 84% of
  the population reliant on public coverage; out-of-pocket spend +10% 2022-23);
  low accessibility (16 specialists and 80 doctors per 100,000; complex family
  logistics); variable quality (deteriorating public system); limited
  transparency (patient confusion, unnecessary consultations); bad service
  experience (long waits, paper records).

## Vision ("Better Health Starts Here")
- Improve access and affordability to quality healthcare in South Africa;
  become the leading pharmacy retailer AND a leading healthcare authority;
  create a sustainable, disruptive, integrated health ecosystem combining
  retail, wholesale and healthcare assets.

## The flywheel (5 building blocks)
1. Affordable health funding products (lowest price point in SA; affordability
   cross-funded from front-shop and script margin; unlocks upper Loyalty 2.0 tier).
2. Digital health platform (guides patients to right point of care; e-visits;
   provider portal; direct scripting; PMS integration).
3. Curated network of providers (owned in-store clinics, hospital groups,
   strategic/affiliate partners, individual GPs; top-of-license operation).
4. Omnichannel retail & pharmacy assets (automatic/'one-click' script
   fulfilment to nearest Dis-Chem or TLC; front-shop benefits from health
   traffic and acts as recruitment engine into the ecosystem).
5. Comprehensive integrated data (360-degree EHR across the ecosystem via PMS).

## Target customers
- Four segments by income/coverage: Zandile (well-served executive, ~60% of
  private health spend, ~5% of population), Tebogo (chronic manager, ~20%/~15%),
  Lethu (young parent retail worker, ~15%/~35%), Busi (informal trader,
  ~5%/~50%). The priority: Lethu and Busi type segments — the poorly-served
  and not-served ~50% of the population.

## Scope choices
- "Health universe" mapped from core health to societal health; explicit
  play/partner/exclude choices per care setting. Funding products prioritise
  primary care & diagnostics, outpatient specialist, in-patient hospital, and
  medication. Adjacent-health funding initially excluded — "to be confirmed
  with customer research".

## Better Rewards as integrator
- "Overall integration delivered through Better Rewards" — loyalty is the
  connective tissue of the ecosystem (repeated in every later SOTF deck).
