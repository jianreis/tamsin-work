## Summary

The main single-use alternative to a returnable cool box: a 100% corrugated
cardboard parcel shipper with plastic-free "Thermaflute" insulation that the
patient drops in household recycling. Vendor claims thermal performance close to
expanded polystyrene and up to 72 hours hold time. The same company also sells
reusable vacuum-panel shippers with a retest-and-reuse programme aimed at B2B
pharma, not consumers. Vendor marketing, tier C. Direct fetch blocked on
2026-09-07; claims from search extracts of the release and its syndications.

## Key claims

- Tempcell ECO launched in the US January 2020; announced 30 June 2020.
- Made from 100% (recycled) corrugated cardboard with patent-pending
  Thermaflute plastic-free insulation; flat-packed in four sizes.
- Disposable in commercial or household kerbside recycling.
- Vendor claims insulation similar to traditional EPS shippers and internal
  temperature maintenance for up to 72 hours (⚠️ vendor figure, profile
  unstated).
- Softbox also markets reusable VIP parcel shippers with a "retest and reuse"
  programme for pharma distributors.
- Recognised in Fast Company's 2020 World Changing Ideas awards (honourable
  mention).
