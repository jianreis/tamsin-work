## Summary

Refrigerated pharmacy lockers are a real product category, not a concept. Multiple
European and US vendors sell units that hold 2-8°C compartments alongside ambient
compartments, with continuous monitoring, and quote outdoor ambient operating
envelopes to +50°C. Indicative pricing is roughly EUR 8,000-18,000 per unit, with
payback claims in the 12-18 month range.

Treat every number here as vendor marketing. There is no independent field data in
this material on how the units actually perform, no published excursion rate, and
no South African deployment. The value of the source is narrow and real: it settles
the question of whether the hardware exists, and it puts an order of magnitude on
what a cold-chain locker rail would cost per site before power, service and
pharmacist oversight.

## Key claims

- Refrigerated pharmacy lockers maintain **+2°C to +8°C** compartments for vaccines
  and biologics, alongside ambient compartments for standard medicines.
- Indicative cost **EUR 8,000-18,000 per unit** for 2-8°C capability. Vendor figure.
- Claimed **50-55% last-mile delivery cost saving** and **12-18 month payback**.
  Vendor figure, unverified.
- Outdoor units are specified to operate in ambient conditions from about
  **-25°C to +50°C**, using powder-coated steel, IP54 or better weather sealing, and
  thermal insulation.
- Refrigerated and freezer configurations use 75-150mm high-density polyurethane
  foam insulation.
- The quoted -25°C to +50°C figures describe the **enclosure and electronics
  operating envelope, not the temperature experienced by a parcel inside a
  non-refrigerated compartment**. No vendor in this set publishes measured internal
  compartment temperatures for an unconditioned locker in direct sun.
