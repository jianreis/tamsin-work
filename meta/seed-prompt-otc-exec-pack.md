## The story as it now stands (post both workshops)
- Decoupling works for scripts; the frontline defends it. The problem is
  OTC-specific: the flow assumes preparation time OTC does not have.
- Friction decays with customer learning (Melrose month 4: "much less
  friction"; 44-second pick perceived as 30 minutes). Much of the OTC pain
  is perceived length + invisibility, plus a real display/sales gap.
- First revenue signal: Melrose OTC turnover R50-100k/month below Blueberry
  Square (lower LSM, similar size) — attributed by its manager to the
  missing display, not the process. Counter-signal: hidden prices ease
  premium upsell.
- Store mixes drift script-ward as stores mature (Melrose 72:28 → ~45:55).
  Irene's 60:40 OTC-heaviness is partly a new-store effect — control for
  maturity in any Melrose-vs-Irene comparison, and do not present Irene's
  mix as a permanent archetype.
- Irene's solve = options A+B fused (dedicated printer #9 + counter
  handover, OTC-only boundary, zero new tech, "no complaints since").
- The binding rollout constraint is staffing: ~1 picker per dispenser (both
  managers independently); small stores (3 staff) cannot run role-split
  processes. Every option must state its headcount precondition.
- Frontline-backed solution: digital OTC planogram on existing screens,
  monetisable as vendor CTU space (revenue line, not cost); interactive
  categorised browse screen as support. Proof of trigger: "oh, I forgot —
  add ACC" at a display depth of two products.
- Market precedent: Medirite and Clicks run single queue + kiosk ticket
  types + OTC routed to PBQs + visible OTC stock. There is no consistent
  OTC standard in legacy Dis-Chem stores (incl. non-compliant handovers).

## Framing rules for the exec audience
- Do NOT use "walk-backs" language (Tamsin's explicit call) — frame as
  evidence-led evolution of the design against its own original intents.
- Score options against the original decision intents (never turn your
  back; production-line fulfilment; space/resourcing) — the pack must show
  the intents are preserved, not abandoned.
- Guardrails to carry: health authority (Clint's cue argument); never trade
  off the urgently sick customer; script flow untouched; cheap and
  reversible (ROWA ~100-107 stores from next year dissolves the problem;
  ~20 manual stores open before then and need the bridge).
- Options include "run the two-store experiment and measure" (~3 months,
  active data plan) as a legitimate recommendation, not an absence of one.
- Honesty about evidence tiers: manager-quoted figures are working numbers
  until pulled from reporting (Carol Mazaris has visit-ID reporting).
- Style: `meta/writing-style-guide.md` governs prose; the `tamsins-voice`
  skill governs the exec register. Concept-card/moments conventions in
  CLAUDE.md §8 apply where relevant.
