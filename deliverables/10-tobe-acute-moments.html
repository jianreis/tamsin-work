The acute script, in moments

You shouldn't have to be well to get your medicine. Today's acute journey is designed for a healthy person to navigate - this one is designed for a sick one. From the moment the doctor decides what you need, everything between you and the first dose is Dis-Chem's job, not yours: decided in the room, priced before you commit, checked by name, ready before you are or arriving just after you - and never alone with new medicine. Toggle the economy to walk it as Aisha (insured, her own illness) or Thabo (cash, his son's); open any phase's behind the line for the service blueprint underneath.

Trust > Convenience > Speed  - speed as evidence of care: the burden moves, that's the promise
