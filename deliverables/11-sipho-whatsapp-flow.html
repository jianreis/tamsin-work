Sipho's repeat, as a WhatsApp conversation

The cash track of the household repeat journey (#09), rendered as the literal WhatsApp thread on Sipho's phone - one phone per phase, from sign-up in July through a full August cycle to the months when something breaks. Every message uses a real mechanic of the WhatsApp Business Platform - templates, reply buttons, list sheets, a Flow, voice notes - and every mechanic is chosen to honour his constraints: prepaid data, an entry-level Android, no app, no street address. The phones are live: tap the highlighted rows and dashed pills to walk each conversation. The rail alongside each phone explains what the messages are doing and why. The threads render here in English for this audience; in production they arrive in the language Sipho chose at sign-up.

Trust > Convenience > Speed  - predictability beats speed

A0
Language · standing note
Production renders every thread on this page in the language Sipho picks in this phase, with his mother's medicine instructions in hers. English is shown here for this audience only.
B1
Click-to-chat entry
A poster QR at the clinic or a saved number - the opening "Hi" is prefilled, so sign-up starts with zero typing, no app store, no account.
B2
List message · language first
Before anything else is asked, the thread's language becomes his - one sheet, one tap, six languages.
B3
WhatsApp Flow · native form
The one place a form beats chat: household and medicines on a native sheet, no webview, no data-heavy page. Multi-screen in production; one screen shown here ⚠️. Consent is an already-granted fact whose scope is stated - ordering, paying, collecting - never an in-flow event (POPIA: recorded once, in-language, auditable).
B4
Costed sync plan
Script Sync made visible: the one top-up that lands both cycles on payday is priced in rand before he says yes - never charged into an agreement ⚠️.
B5
Read-back + reply-1 confirm
Everything he chose, read back in plain language, confirmed with a single character - the only confirmation gate in the whole journey, because it is the consent moment.
Key momentat B5
I told them once how I want it
One WhatsApp conversation, a few minutes - and it's remembered: not asked again at a counter, not to a driver, not at the bank. The CarerConsent concept, on the phone he already owns.

What each message is doing

markers (A1-A5) match the callouts · feature chips name the WhatsApp mechanic · ⚠️ marks claims awaiting a vault source

A1
Utility template · business-initiated
The one-week-out notice. Text only - no image header, no PDF - because on a prepaid balance every download costs real money (small-bundle data carries a poverty premium per MB). It names the day because this order's day is genuinely settled, and it closes on the golden path: nothing to do.
Key moment⭐ moment of truthat A1
My medicine ordered itself
He reads it and puts the phone away - nothing to install, nothing to spend, nothing to remember. The family's supply stays ahead of run-out, on the phone he already owns. The Auto-Refill concept with no app in the way.
A2
Itemised price · in-bubble text
The price lives in the message, not at the till. His mother's CCMDD parcel shows as R0.00 in the same breakdown - covered is stated, never hidden. Amounts shown are illustrative, not sourced SEP calculations ⚠️.
Key moment⭐ moment of truthat A2
The only thing I ever bought blind - not anymore
He knows whether the trip is affordable before he decides to travel - not at the till, after the taxi fare is already spent. Real-Time Transparency, for the majority who pay cash.
A3
Interactive reply buttons · max 3
WhatsApp allows exactly three quick-reply buttons, and the design spends them on change, pause, person. There is no confirm button on purpose: silence is the golden path, and the order carries on by itself ⚠️.
A4
List message · up to 10 rows
Behind "Change something": a native list sheet - menu depth without menu typing. The last row keeps free text first-class, so the flow never traps him in options that don't fit. "Add medicine to this order" is P2's doorway, present here only as a row ⚠️. (tap Change something in the phone to see it)
A5
Keyword reply + phone-back
Escalation ends in a phone call, not a chat queue: one reply, and a person phones him in his language, free of charge. The closing line matters most on prepaid - he can let his data lapse, because the call comes to him. (tap Talk to a person in the phone to see it)

What each message is doing

markers (C1-C4) match the callouts

C1
Numbered picks · specials first
Suggestions come from his own buying history with anything on special at the top - and they're answered by typing a digit. A number works on any handset, at any literacy level, and costs one character of airtime.
C2
Free text · one clarifying question
Typing "cough mixture" is as good as any menu. The service asks exactly one question back - child or adult - as reply buttons, then returns priced options. Never a form, never a dead end.
C3
Running total · restated every change
Price-before-travel survives the additions: after every item the total he'll pay at the Capitec counter is restated in full ⚠️.
C4
Honest constraint · medicine only
The locker route carries medicine only, no groceries - stated plainly up front, exactly as #09 requires, instead of discovered at the locker.

No key moment in this phase by design - #09 agreed the cash addition carries none. The value is quieter: the small pharmacy trips between paydays fold into the one he already makes.

E1
Reminder template · the whole trip in one message
Amount and PIN restated together, before he leaves home - so the trip needs nothing further from the network. If signal fades at the rank, the message is already on the phone.

The handover itself never touches the chat: cash at the Capitec counter releases the locker on his PIN - the Pelebox pattern near home, no street address required. E3
Photo reply -> a person phones
The contents list lands right after handover so the parcel is checked at the locker, not at home after the taxi. A photo in the same thread brings a person to the phone at once ⚠️. (tap the photo pill in the phone to see it)

The same certainty, differently shaped: payment and collection in one visit, near home, with no address required - and if the locker is ever down, he knows before he travels, not after.

What each message is doing

markers (F1-F4) match the callouts

F1
List message · the medicine cabinet
Every medicine the family takes, one sheet away - what it's for, how to take it - with zero downloads. The answer no longer lives at a counter across town.
F2
Voice notes · both directions
When reading isn't enough, he asks in the thread - typed or spoken. Voice carries the literacy range, and it's his mother's channel as much as his: her reminders arrive on her phone as short voice notes in her language.
F3
STOP · honoured instantly
One keyword ends the reminders, completely and immediately. The research is blunt about why: the same tools that reduce effort flip into harassment the moment agency is lost.
F4
The free floor lives in the parcel
Iconised labels, the printed day-by-day fridge sheet, instructions in each person's language - the adherence basics ride with the medicine, not the phone. The chat carries only the look-up and the pharmacist.
Key momentat F4
Medication you can't take wrong
The basics done properly: a clear label on every pack, icons that speak before words do, a sheet on the fridge, a guide on the phone. Taking it right isn't something money buys - it comes with the medicine. The promise Made-for-Me Medicine makes at the paid end, kept at the free end too.

What each message is doing

markers (G1-G4) match the callouts · scenarios, not steps - each is a different month

G1
Exception template · the plan attached
Bad news never arrives alone: the message carries the live choices as reply buttons, and nothing is decided without him. An exception opens a path - it never kills the order.
G2
Told before travelling
The taxi-fare rule: anything knowable before he travels - a broken locker, a stock-out, a waiting renewal - is told before he travels. His fare is never spent on a closed door.
G3
Script Renew · signposted
The renewal journey starts weeks before the script runs dry, visibly, in the same thread - and chasing it costs him nothing. The full renewal flow is its own journey (the Script Renew concept), out of scope here.
G4
A person, by phone
When things truly break, the human comes by phone, one reply away, in his language - the loud human-not-bot beat the sentiment research demands.

No key moment on the cash track by design: #09's ∥ moment of truth is medical-aid-specific and insured-only. The cash recovery paths carry the same care without a separate memory line.
