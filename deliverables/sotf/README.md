# SOTF deliverables

Store of the Future (SOTF) project outputs live here, separated from the
Future of Scripting deliverables in the parent folder (`01`-`13`, which are
preserved as they are per vault etiquette).

Ground SOTF work in the programme hub page,
`wiki/dischem/store-of-the-future-programme.md`, and cite the vault as usual.
Commit messages: `vault: sotf — <what changed>`.

Contents:

- `otc-solve-session.html` (+ `.pptx`) - the 2 Sep 2026 internal OTC solve
  workshop canvas. Preserved as run; not reworked in place.
- `otc-exec-pack-spec.md` - structure, slide plan and draft copy for the
  executive / Steer Group pack on the OTC solve (Sep 2026). Spec first; the
  HTML mirrors it once each section's copy is agreed.
- `otc-exec-pack.html` - v1 of the pack (four-question structure with the full
  future-state design in section 3), preserved; `otc-exec-pack.pptx` is its
  editable twin, generated by `otc-exec-pack.build.js`.
- `otc-exec-pack-v2.html` - **v2, the simple version (current)**: the ask, what
  changed, where OTC hurts, two stores, three options, pros and cons, the hybrid
  recommendation, what is coming anyway, what we measure; appendix carries the
  roadmap, the parked future-state thinking and sources. Spec:
  `otc-exec-pack-v2-spec.md`. Editable twin `otc-exec-pack-v2.pptx` from
  `otc-exec-pack-v2.build.js`; caveats and working-number notes in speaker notes.
