# OTC solve - executive pack v2

Why a v2 (Tamsin, 2026-09-17, after a conversation with her partner): v1's
section 3 turned a decision pack into a design pack, and section 4 then had
to carry that design. v2 puts the decision first and parks the design
thinking in the appendix. Frame: the business asked us to address a
perceived OTC problem; the evidence is anecdotal; scripts are fine; there
are three options for OTC (do nothing and get data; roll back OTC to a
coupled counter; a hybrid inspired by Irene); pros and cons; recommend the
hybrid with named changes; say what we measure. Tamsin's addition: one slide
on the future pieces (OCR at the counter, script scan at the kiosk, drop and
shop, express collect) and how each reduces congestion and wait.

v1 (`otc-exec-pack.html`, `.pptx`, `otc-exec-pack-spec.md`) is preserved
unchanged. Its section 3 survives here as appendix material only.

## Slide 8 grounding (the future pieces)

- OCR at the counter / script scan at the kiosk: in-store digital script
  submission was deferred from the Melrose MVP to H2 2026; OCR script
  checkout is in the app rebuild [[src-sotf-melrose-workshops-2026]]; the
  scripting roadmap anchors automated capture and BRIX
  [[src-dc-omnichannel-scripting-roadmap]].
- Drop and shop: decoupling as designed, "roaming time drives front-shop
  traffic" [[src-sotf-steerco1-concept-2025]]; ~80% of Irene customers do not
  want to shop, so the piece that helps is the option to leave and come back
  [[src-sotf-otc-frontline-workshop-2026]].
- Express collect: the FoS concept set, `deliverables/08-concepts.html`
  (concept "Express Collect"); collections from any counter already live at
  Irene.
