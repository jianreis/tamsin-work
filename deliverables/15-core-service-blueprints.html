What has to run behind 09: Repeat and 10: Acute

Two high-level service blueprints, one for each future-state journey. Each one shows what the customer does and sees in every phase, then what has to happen behind the line for that to work: the people and processes backstage, the technology and data underneath, and the rules that apply. Below each blueprint is a capability roll-up of what must be in place, organised by the eight capability rows of the work-to-be-done catalogue (#14), so the two documents can be read side by side. A last section separates the foundation both journeys share from what each needs alone.

The blueprints sit one level above the journeys. Where the insured and cash tracks differ, the cell says so with a INS or CASH tag. The detail behind each cell lives in the journey's own behind-the-line panels: #09 and #10.
